The existing TRAIN archive, not future TEST quality, supplies the byte estimates. It has 960 unique rows and 53,852,424 bytes per results copy. Context journals and aggregate results are both retained by the frozen runner. The prepared 302 input graphs occupy 1,118,492,687 uncompressed bytes and are already included in the measured server usage.

`experiments/analysis/v06/performance_storage_metadata_v06_001.json` records every track/target distribution; its SHA-256 is `5724ba51d2f78deb086b83f33a9d841f1ef58b8bc72edadcfb6d21484c218c7b`. The separate `performance_storage_extrapolation_v06_001.json` scales Degree row sizes to all 23 future policies on 302 contexts, preserving both cold/warm tracks and .1/1/5 targets. The two results copies estimate 11.315 GB at the TRAIN means, 31.142 GB in the all-groups p95 scenario, and 36.067 GB in the all-groups observed-max scenario. These are sensitivity scenarios, not prediction intervals or formal bounds. LLM feature cost, larger graphs, initial greedy traces and changing patch counts can alter the distribution. No TEST optimizer or scorer was used to derive them.

The data disk is XFS with approximately 30 GB initially free; it has no transparent compression, and `/dev/fuse` is absent. The system overlay has only approximately 5 GB free and is not a substitute output destination. A hard maximum cannot be inferred from the TRAIN row-size maximum. The existing 512-patch cap and all complete traces remain unchanged.

Before launch, root preserves complete old, inactive, root-owned workspace directories in a separate lossless archive and verifies all file hashes against a full downloaded local backup. Only verified redundant uncompressed copies may then be removed. The current EoH workspace, prepared V003 inputs, Python runtime, executable baselines and all canonical research archives remain in place. The preservation backup is private under `.research`, since full historical working directories can include auxiliary source and transport records beyond the publicly curated result archives. This is not deletion of experimental evidence or a claim that old result tars automatically cover a complete workspace.

The prelaunch free-space measurement, preservation manifest/local verification and cleanup receipt will be attached before actual solver execution. Reserve at least 20% of measured free capacity when judging the mean-based allocation and final compressed-archive peak. The p95/max scenarios remain visible as risks, not silently converted into certified bounds. Root monitors bytes and free space during the run. There is no live removal of journals, trace truncation, result compression hook, frozen-runner edit, policy resampling, or hidden repeat. Any actual failure or original whole-batch guard remains part of the full predeclared assignment frame.

This engineering preflight is supporting material, not a paper result. The complete original byte-level archives, failures, cost receipts and metadata remain required for release.
