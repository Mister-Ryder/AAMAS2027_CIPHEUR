## Components and contribution

The new deployment kernel uses a common Degree initializer, bounded
multi-vertex independent-set replacement, classical clique-cover bounds and
exact local branch-and-bound. These are established solver techniques. They
provide feasibility, retain an incumbent at a budget stop, and improve the
available search neighborhood. They do not establish a new framework
contribution or a faithful reproduction of CHILS, M2WIS or Struction.

The framework contribution remains conditional-value evidence under controlled
constraint interventions, exact feature-equivalence obstruction detection,
typed feature/rule synthesis directed by concrete joins, and joint selection
for numerical specification fit, complete-schedule quality and measured cost.
All synthesis arms share the same classical kernel. The frozen program changes
only a local feasible greedy proposal and the pivot vertex. It does not change
bounds, the shared include-first traversal, feasibility or the conditional-value
certification procedure. Classical bounded search is disclosed explicitly;
there is no online LLM or conditional-value certification service.

The paper should explain these mature components briefly, cite their context,
and use space for the framework's question and evidence. Detailed operational
limits, provenance, failed trials and artifact checks belong in this report and
the companion Markdown documents.

## Intended presentation and its limit

The new first experimental table should present actual information repair,
scalar fit, withheld reversal/preservation behavior and cost. A separate full
published-method comparison must retain all requested solvers, their citations,
raw quality, valid-incumbent coverage and observed time. High performance from
shared mature components must not be attributed to witness guidance. A gain in
information repair, sample efficiency or computation is an empirical hypothesis
until the new results support it. Final paper/release work remains pending.
