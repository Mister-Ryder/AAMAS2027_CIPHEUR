## Seven concise citation placements

These are suggestions for the root editor; no manuscript section was edited. Together they give every new entry an actual role without a long citation string or a bibliography-only inclusion.

1. **Introduction, domain motivation.** After the first discussion of shared satellite/ground resources: “Constellation planning couples scarce space and ground resources with offline scheduling and limited onboard computation~\cite{picard2021constellations}.” The challenges paper supplies domain context; keep our restricted pairwise model explicit elsewhere.

2. **Related work, graph search foundations.** After the specialized published MWIS comparators: “Independent-set exchange neighborhoods~\cite{andrade2012local} and coloring-based clique bounds~\cite{ostergard2002clique} are classical search tools; our certificates use such tools to establish conditional ranking evidence.” Keep “classical exchange” labelled as a project control rather than renaming it ARW.

3. **Related work, adaptive heuristic choice and computation.** “Dynamic heuristic selection conditions choices on search state~\cite{speck2021dynamic}, while algorithm configuration optimizes settings over instance distributions and accounts for evaluation time~\cite{hutter2009paramils}.” Follow with the specific distinction: our offline synthesizer refines which graph distinctions a frozen scorer receives. ParamILS supplies parameter-configuration and evaluation-cost precedent, not policy-synthesis guarantees.

4. **Related work, LLM algorithm design.** Add after EoH/ReEvo: “LLaMEA evolves executable metaheuristics using empirical fitness feedback~\cite{vanstein2025llamea}.” This expands formal published coverage. It supplies precedent, not a new benchmark cell.

5. **Related work, representation and strategy obligations.** “Generalized planning changes feature/action representations~\cite{bonet2018representation}; exact strategy synthesis also distinguishes state classification from executable action conditions~\cite{fang2024generalized}.” Then connect our full-interface DAG/scalar-rule distinction while noting the different planning/game semantics.

6. **Method or related work, typed synthesis contract.** “Verifier-guided sketching~\cite{solarlezama2006sketch} and syntax-guided synthesis~\cite{alur2013sygus} separate constrained candidate syntax from behavioral specifications.” Our paragraph should immediately state that full-interface acyclicity is a finite information test and does not certify the bounded rule or unseen schedules.

7. **Related work, closest formal synthesis distinction.** “Abstraction-guided synthesis refines program semantics to eliminate spurious candidates~\cite{wang2018abstraction}, and relational synthesis constrains relationships among calls or programs~\cite{wang2018relational}.” Our paper instead obtains sound conditional decision inequalities from scheduling instances and checks whether the input quotient can express them. This is the substantive gap to explain; generic refinement or relational constraints alone are not novel.
