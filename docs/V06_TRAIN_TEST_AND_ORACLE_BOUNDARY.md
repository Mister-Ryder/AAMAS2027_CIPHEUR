# V06 TRAIN, TEST and oracle boundary

## Direct answer

Offline certificates and schedule fitness use the **TRAIN graphs** to construct
and select programs. That is training, and their TRAIN fit/quality must not be
called generalization. The frozen program is subsequently executed on other
registered states. In the inspected frozen paths, TEST conditional values are
not inputs to its scoring or scheduling kernel.

Two evaluation frames require a further distinction. The 72-state mechanism
study certifies withheld preferences **after the authoring/selection freeze**
to measure their transfer. The 302-context weighted performance study has no
conditional-certificate input at all. Its optional strong initialization is a
new, budgeted CHILS computation on the current graph, not an offline solution
label. This source-flow clarification does not rerun an audit, oracle, solver
or authoring request, and does not establish that every public dataset was
historically unseen.

## What is learned and when

The R2 authoring packets contain a shared TRAIN-derived warm seed, selected
TRAIN graph examples, TRAIN fit/quality feedback, and arm-specific labels or
equality joins. Assessment uses the frozen 120-state TRAIN inventory for fit,
representation consistency, quality and work. Selecting a program using this
information is permitted training. The fixed-catalogue minimum is similarly
a result on its retained TRAIN evidence, not a TEST guarantee.

The final R2 selection freezes four genuine W-joint programs and separate
quality-only comparator roles. The registered performance deployment binds
that exact selection and the independently frozen EoH selection. It retains
all four EoH outputs' common seed origin; those identities are not four new
learned programs. No TEST-best program or source is substituted after evaluation.

The main TEST release records `before_any_TEST_labels_or_performance=true`,
TRAIN-only selection and the completed authoring/assessment barrier. The
heldout adapter validates this release before reading certificates. It then
requires each TEST certificate execution/completion to bind
`programme_freeze_sha256` to the same release, plus the original graph/query
inventory. Thus the certificate target is evaluation of already frozen
identities, not feedback for subsequent proposal or selection in this study.

## The 72-state mechanism evaluator

The adapter retains all 72 original withheld states and their fixed queries.
Saved sound bounds produce evaluation labels for strict rankings, exact ties,
reversal/preservation and full-quotient consistency. Identifier mappings are
also fixed and preserve the same underlying source/query dependence.

These labels are available to the **evaluation controller**, which compares
scores against them. They are not passed to `repair_schedule`. The saved
kernel call supplies only the current graph, frozen program, fixed/excluded
boundary, priority choice and declared budget/configuration. The graph and
boundary are the scheduling input; no preferred action, conditional optimum,
certificate margin or witness join is a runtime argument. The registered
program entries remain unchanged while their scores and schedules are measured.

This is a legitimate postfreeze preference-transfer evaluation, not proof of
a schedule-quality gain. All 13,392 kernel positions tie matched Degree in
reward. Full-residual evaluation labels also do not automatically describe
patch-local choices: the separate TRAIN bridge explicitly measures the
different outside-fixed patch boundary. It does not retrofit labels into
deployment or change TEST programs.

## The 302-context weighted performance driver

`scripts/run_performance_r2_eoh_test_v06.py:345` reads a hash-bound original
graph and the frozen 23-identity deployment. In `run_context`, native methods
receive the graph, fixed seed and nominal target. Policy execution at
lines 408--410 receives the graph, frozen program, fixed repair configuration,
time budget, and either no imported incumbent or the shared initializer's
selected set. No certificate archive or conditional-label argument occurs
in this execution path.

The cold track starts from common Degree initialization. The warm track
computes **CHILS seed 1 for T/2 on the current graph**, then repairs its checked
feasible incumbent for the remaining nominal T/2. The incumbent is an actual
classical optimization result, not an optimality oracle or certified preference
label. A failed initializer makes the associated warm request unavailable;
there is no hidden fallback. Actual initialization is reused across policies,
but every reported standalone warm cost includes its full measured native
initialization cost plus that policy's repair cost. Reuse is not free compute
or a claim of equal end-to-end hard deadlines.

The repair kernel still performs ordinary budgeted optimization, including
local branch-and-bound and clique bounds. Typed feature reductions may compute
greedy packings or clique covers. These are explicit, charged algorithms on
the current graph; “no conditional oracle” does not mean “no online search.”
It means the offline full-residual conditional-completion certification service
and its stored action-preference labels are absent from deployment.

## Were public performance instances used in calibration?

The necessary metadata comparison uses the two frozen protocol source lists,
without opening new quality outcomes. The V06 component TRAIN calibration has
32 original public source IDs; the final performance protocol has 38 original
public IDs. Their intersection is **zero**. The earlier public TRAIN calibration
uses the 25 WDP TRAIN sources plus the three Segmentation TRAIN sources, all
contained in that 32-source set.

| Original public source family | V06 component TRAIN calibration | V003 public performance |
|---|---|---|
| WDP | 25 TRAIN source IDs | Other 25 source IDs from the fixed source split |
| Segmentation | 12, 13, 16 | 14, 18, 19 |
| Grids | 25, 28, 29 | 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 26, 27, 30 |
| ProteinFolding | 11 | None |

This rules out “the same original instance as those two V06 calibration
studies” for the registered public performance inputs. It does **not** establish
whole-corpus invisibility: public raw inputs and metadata were downloaded and
accessible during development, and the framework was developed using other
public graphs and newly induced states. The appropriate description is a
fixed public source-split benchmark comparison, with exact original objectives
and encoding limits, not a corpus-unseen claim.

The 216 newly generated weighted scheduling contexts provide the clearer
fresh-instance performance frame. The 72 newly constructed mechanism states
measure state/constraint-level transfer within their explicitly stated
source-derived/synthetic scope. The 48 C3 performance contexts are previously
exposed, separate exploratory models and must not be pooled into a fresh
generalization claim. The inspected bindings reveal no prior TEST conditional
oracle feedback entering these frozen programs; this is a bounded source-flow
finding, not a new global forensic audit of all historical access.
