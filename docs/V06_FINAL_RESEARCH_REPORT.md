# V06 research checkpoint report

## Delivery and scientific position

The V06 delivery comprises the runnable frozen framework, AAMAS manuscript,
authoring records, classical-method comparisons and canonical server evidence.
The paper artifact is [CIPHEUR_AAMAS2027_V06.pdf](../paper/CIPHEUR_AAMAS2027_V06.pdf),
with public release target [v0.6.0](https://github.com/Mister-Ryder/AAMAS2027_CIPHEUR/releases/tag/v0.6.0).
[Replay instructions](REPLAY_V06.md) describe evidence restoration and analysis.
The release does not imply a guarantee of conference acceptance.

The V06 results, independent verification and statistical analyses are complete. Its checkpoint manuscript emphasizes the information-repair contribution; this report retains the full favorable, null and unfavorable findings. The intended large-scale deployment advantage remains unestablished. V07 therefore starts with [mathematical scene-selection criteria](V07_DATA_SELECTION_MATHEMATICAL_CRITERIA.md) and the [TRAIN contribution-chain diagnosis](V07_TRAIN_CONTRIBUTION_CHAIN_DIAGNOSIS.md), before new performance comparisons. Public Actions status applies only to revisions actually pushed.

The central contribution is a method for distinguishing an insufficient
representation from an insufficient ranking rule. Sound conditional-completion
bounds produce strict action preferences or exact ties under explicit boundary
commitments. Equal demanded feature vectors merge actions into a quotient;
a directed cycle proves that no scalar scorer on that interface can satisfy
all retained strict requirements. Concrete equality joins then identify which
structural distinctions a proposal must add. The feature interface and scalar
rule are validated and selected together with complete-schedule quality and
feature work, within a fixed feasibility-enforcing classical kernel.

The LLM supplies a conditionable proposal prior over bounded typed compositional
programs. A certificate diagnoses missing information but does not itself
construct a typed distinguishing expression and compatible rule. Deterministic
validation retains authority over admissibility, and offline authoring cost is
amortized over deployment of a frozen program. This explains the design choice;
the theory does not prove that an LLM is necessary, cheaper or better than
enumeration. The completed finite-catalogue experiment demonstrates a viable
deterministic alternative once a suitable catalogue is available. See the
[design rationale](V06_LLM_DESIGN_RATIONALE.md).

## Evidence for representation refinement and synthesis

The original R1 study retained 120 attempted positions, of which 112 were valid
and assessed. Only nine of twelve requested joint-selection cells had a genuine
eligible winner. Its original release barrier failed and its proposed matched
TEST was stopped. Later studies do not retroactively change this result.

R2 used the same visible warm seed, bounded library, shared kernel, candidate
limits and deterministic assessment across witness (W), relation-label (R) and
objective-only (O) arms. Fifteen requests produced 120 valid programs, assessed
on 120 TRAIN states each: 14,400 assignments. Joint selection and quality-only
selection are separate roles; a quality-selected comparator is not relabeled
as a certificate-consistent proposed winner.

| R2 outcome | Witness W | Relations R | Objective O |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| All five blocks: joint eligible positions | 28/40 | 24/40 | 0/40 |
| Predeclared first four complete blocks | 26/32 | 19/32 | 0/32 |
| Mean raw strict fit, matched blocks | 547.90625/594 | 574.8125/594 | 552.9375/594 |
| Complete TRAIN macro quality, every candidate | 2693/4480 | 2693/4480 | 2693/4480 |

The fifth retained block favors R (W 2/8, R 5/8), so the matched result is not
presented as universal dominance. All 120 candidates have the same TRAIN
macro reward relative to total input reward; none fits all 594 strict demands.
W's stronger information-gate yield is distinct from rule fit, schedule quality
and work. R has higher mean raw strict fit, and selected W programs do not
establish lower work. Actual authoring tokens and latency are not matched.
Four conditional blocks with a shared seed do not estimate model-population
superiority. Full selection, exact work and coverage are in the
[R2 scientific review](V06_R2_TRAIN_SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW.md).

The independent finite-catalogue study uses all 53 canonical expressions
observed among valid R1 candidates. Four additions make the complete TRAIN
quotient acyclic at exact minimum additive standalone cost **1,371,468**;
a separate necessary-cut lower-bound certificate attains that value. Nine
master/full-quotient checks are required: removing all self-loops still leaves
a genuine two-arc cycle, and repairing that cycle exposes another. SHA-ordered
prefixes of 4, 8 and 16 expressions remain unresolved because they cannot
separate a concrete witness, not because they exceed the search budget.

This is a minimum over an adaptive, fixed 53-expression TRAIN catalogue and
positive additive standalone charges. It is neither runtime optimality,
bounded-scalar fit nor unseen-data invariance. The catalogue itself was
LLM-generated and is not a blind non-LLM authoring control. Detailed proofs,
cost decomposition and negative cases are in
[catalogue findings](V06_CATALOGUE_COST_FINDINGS.md).

## Authentic published synthesis comparator

The EoH-DSL comparator performs 32 genuine sequential single-candidate calls
and four seed fitness evaluations, following the frozen operator schedule,
rank-based parent sampling with replacement and rounded-quality population
tie rule. Fitness uses TRAIN objective feedback, with no certificate/fit labels
or witness feedback. The constrained DSL and shared repair kernel make this a
typed adaptation of the published procedure, not an unrestricted native
reproduction.

All observed TRAIN qualities equal **2693/4480**. All four selected pipeline
positions retain the common R1 warm seed and the same executable AST. They
remain four requested outputs with preserved provenance; they are not four
newly learned programs or independent TEST samples. Served backend model
identity remains unknown where receipts do not disclose it. W-versus-EoH is
a pipeline contrast with different inherited evidence, selectors and authoring
budgets, not a witness-only causal estimate. See
[frozen EoH outcomes](V06_PUBLISHED_EOH_FROZEN_RESULTS.md).

## Heldout transfer and identifier robustness

The final heldout frame retains 27 R2/control identities and four EoH pipeline
identities on 72 states, each under the original numbering and five paired
bijections. All **13,392 kernel positions** are normal and independently
feasible; all matched Degree reward differences are exact zero.

| Original-numbering outcome | W joint | W quality | R quality | O quality | EoH seed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Strict fit | 91.94% | 88.58% | 93.76% | 88.02% | 94.48% |
| Actual base-nine alias fit | 259/316 | 247/316 | 261/316 | 203/316 | 67/79 |
| Both endpoints correct: strict reversal | 43/52 | 43/52 | 45/52 | 9/13 | 11/13 |
| Both endpoints correct: strict preservation | 317/332 | 149/166 | 325/332 | 153/166 | 83/83 |

W-joint strict fit is **+3.37 percentage points** above W-quality, including
the effect of joint selection, but **−1.82 pp** below R-quality. Under the
common quality-only selector, W−R is **−5.19 pp**, with the prepared source
95% interval **[−7.00, −3.50] pp**. Source/pair intervals are conditional on
these frozen identities; they do not convert duplicate origins or four blocks
into independent model draws.

All four W-joint original demanded quotients are acyclic, but three of their
twenty renamed positions become contradictory. Acyclicity on the original
inventory is therefore not an identifier-invariance guarantee. Renamings are
dependent observations within each source, and ties remain separate from
strict failures. The 72-state frame is an alias-enriched unit-weight mechanism
study, not evidence of natural physical obstruction incidence or hard weighted
solver superiority. Every identity, permutation and family-specific cost is
retained in [heldout results](V06_HELDOUT_RESULTS_001.md).

## TRAIN bridge from full evidence to actual patch boundaries

The completed bridge retains 120 original Degree first-patch restrictions,
23 frozen identities and **2,760 scored positions**, with no missing identity,
score cap or program error. The 159 distinct conditional queries contain
15 local strict preferences and 144 exact ties. The unbiased core stratum
contains 155 queries: 14 strict and 141 ties; the seven surviving original
full-strict pairs are a separately conditioned stratum and overlap the core.

Degree fits all **15/15** local strict union labels; W-joint fits **58/60**
repeated program-by-label positions. R-quality, O-quality and the EoH seed fit
all their **60/60** repeated positions. These observations supply no W
local-priority advantage. Among seven originally full-strict comparisons,
three retain their direction under the local boundary, one reverses and three
become exact local ties. A sound full-residual certificate cannot automatically
become a patch-local label or proof of realized pivot benefit. The bridge is
a TRAIN scoring diagnostic, not another scheduling run. See
[patch-bridge results](V06_PATCH_BRIDGE_RESULTS.md).

## Larger frozen performance study

The independent final audit covers **52,548 assigned positions**, **302
contexts**, **23 frozen policies**, two initialization tracks and nominal
targets **0.1, 1 and 5 seconds**. It completes **71,184,835 checks with zero
errors**, bound to `performance_TEST_audit_v06_003.json`, SHA-256
`db7209ad60c4d2f1cff07ec50b2bf26fd5d473153c56d809ff416f6781de22d6`.
This establishes saved-evidence consistency and complete coverage, not a
performance advantage.

The prepared single analysis is complete at
`experiments/analysis/v06/performance_TEST_analysis_v06_003/analysis.json`,
SHA-256 **`839324710784ba71ee40c25ea199b527ff346fb7ec1209f97756ed2d9513aaf9`**.
The complete small [performance report](../experiments/analysis/v06/performance_tables_v06_002/PERFORMANCE_RESULTS_V06.md)
retains all fifteen family/population groups at all three targets and all
method estimates. The 897,176,638-byte analysis and large identity outputs
are supplied in lossless parts; exact restoration commands are in
[REPLAY_V06.md](REPLAY_V06.md).

At the declared five-second target, **every defined non-C3 same-source warm
W-joint contrast against Degree or EoH is exact zero**. The shared initializer
accounts for substantial cold-to-warm quality differences; these cannot be
attributed to the LLM. Standard scheduling families tie within the cold track
at T5, while cold W-joint does not improve dense scheduling. In dense-long
ground-scarce instances, its actual paired difference against Degree is
**−0.451%**, with source 95% interval **[−0.611%, −0.283%]**. The final
performance study establishes no general W scheduling-quality advantage.

Coverage matters directly in public weighted families. For WDP_5xx at T5,
the warm W-joint displayed gain relative to CHILS seed 1 is +0.0000% on only
**2/4 complete source groups**, whereas Degree/EoH have −0.3573% on **4/4**.
The genuine matched-source contrast is zero; the apparent table advantage
is a coverage difference, not a method gain. W-joint completes 14/16 individual
members there. On Grids at T5 it completes 30/40 members, but each of ten sources
lacks a complete four-member cohort, so its main quality is **undefined,
0/10 complete groups**. An available-member diagnostic cannot replace that
missing full-member result.

The complete assignment frame retains **525 native encoding-unsupported
positions** and **82 policy errors**. Null quality is not filled with zero,
removed, replaced or turned into a successful baseline comparison. Incompatible
integer widths do not establish weak optimizer performance. The compatible
UAI subsets do not stand for all 81 originally inventoried UAI inputs.

Observed work and runtime do not supply general guidance efficiency either.
For example, at T5 in the standard-balanced warm track, Degree and W-joint
both match the fixed reference reward, while mean measured standalone wall
time is **3.304378 s** for Degree and **4.246202 s** for W-joint. These receipts
charge each policy's complete shared initializer and repair; they are not
authoring cost or a pure feature-time benchmark. Full measurement coverage
and all other families remain in the supporting report.

The prepared analysis keeps fresh scheduling, WDP, compatible UAI-derived
families and previously exposed C3 models separate. Native seeds are averaged
within source, full-member and available-member outcomes remain distinct,
and unsuccessful or unsupported quality is null. The fixed reference is CHILS
full-target seed 1; it is never replaced by the TEST-best solver or seed.
CHILS, ILS, M2WIS, Struction and WeightedBR are mature published infrastructure,
with exact encoding compatibility and measured timing coverage. Equal nominal
targets are not equal end-to-end deadlines, and warm per-policy standalone
cost charges the common initializer in full. See the
[runner design](V06_003_PERFORMANCE_RUNNER_DESIGN.md) and
[analysis contract](V06_PERFORMANCE_ANALYSIS_001.md).

## Paper evidence versus engineering record

The paper contains the scoped quotient argument, typed synthesis mechanism,
relevant comparisons, complete experimental denominators and actual positive,
negative and null findings. The following remain supporting records rather
than additional scientific contributions:

- Original request failures, incomplete selection cells, transport usage and
  unknown served-model identity; R1's failed barrier remains immutable.
- Linux snapshot-path compatibility corrections and append-only provenance
  clarifications, including preserved failed preassessment attempts.
- Archive extraction, lossless multipart packaging, release-asset retrieval,
  storage preflight and host-specific binary paths.
- Audit schema adapters, parser namespace corrections, and process
  parallelization of unchanged verification mathematics.

The parallel audit's check count measures verification scope, not experimental
sample size, algorithm speedup or a confidence level. Its actual method and
unchanged mathematics are documented in
[parallel audit scope](V06_PARALLEL_PERFORMANCE_AUDIT.md). Saved experimental
CPU/wall receipts are not replaced by audit execution timings. Canonical raw
archives, source capsules, author receipts and original failed records retain
their hashes; analysis reads evidence without rerunning authoring or solvers.

The supported LLM contribution is structured program proposal under certified
information constraints. Current evidence does not establish necessary LLM
use, general model superiority, witness dominance in scalar fit, guaranteed
schedule-quality gains, identifier invariance, natural physical obstruction
incidence or conference acceptance.
