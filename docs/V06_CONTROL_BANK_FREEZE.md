Date: 2026-10-03. The control generator performs static schema/grammar validation only. It reads no graph, label, old program bank or raw new author response; it calls no feature evaluator, interface assessment, kernel, oracle or LLM. The controller has already audited TRAIN evidence, so this is a transparent post-evidence/pre-assessment template design, not a claim of blind human or non-AI provenance.

`scripts/prepare_controls_v06.py` creates an immutable inventory at `experiments/discovery/v06_controls_001/control_banks.json`, SHA256 **79effeaff504fb76995c0493f53551b668441f82e9e8a0bb1b4542e14ce3cba8**. The adjacent freeze receipt binds generator, static independent validator and every deployment AST. No existing evidence, response or study result was modified.

The bank has eight structural templates and eight base-nine fixed-interface rules. Structural templates use deterministic catalogue prefixes of two, three, four and six features, each with two fixed blends (0.25/0.75). The catalogue contains greedy independent and clique-cover weight summaries of the neighbor set and forced-action residual, plus neighbor/residual induced-edge counts. Every feature is polynomial and drawn from the same permitted typed library, within six-feature/48-node/eight-depth limits. The six-feature entries expose the maximum permitted feature capacity rather than constraining every enumerated program to a smaller interface. Catalogue-size variation supports cost comparisons; no coefficient, feature or template was chosen by its observed fit or reward.

The base-nine bank contains weight, weight/degree, weight/conflict weight, conflict-weight penalty, high-degree priority, compatible-weight priority, weight/duration and a constraint-gap-adjusted degree expression. A known Degree priority is also supplied as a separate fixed kernel reference. Some base-nine rules coincide in ranking on unit-weight/unit-duration graphs; distinct syntax is not independent semantic evidence. The base-nine representation's inability to fit strict aliases is an information result, not an LLM benefit.

All sixteen original slots must receive the same frozen TRAIN evidence, demanded-interface gate, fit/quality/work selector and shared-kernel cap as model-authored candidates. Keep missing eligibility and every raw slot; do not add replacements after assessment. The controller must bind the final kernel/protocol hashes in its execution capsule, because those owned modules were still under review when the control ASTs were frozen.

If these exact eight ASTs are repeated across four blocks, explicitly report **eight fixed templates assessed repeatedly**, rather than 32 independent proposals or four authoring draws. Repetition captures timing/budget variability. The control is **enumerated and matched in assessed-slot count**; it is not randomized generation, matched token/model compute, an independently human-authored bank or an end-to-end pipeline without supervision. Experimental LLM authoring calls for this comparator are zero, while the wider software/templates were written by the research controller.

A strong result against these controls can demonstrate that new authoring discovered more effective/cost-aware feature–rule combinations than this small frozen catalogue. It cannot establish superiority over arbitrary enumeration or every non-LLM synthesis algorithm. Complete public solver comparisons remain a separate practical evaluation.
