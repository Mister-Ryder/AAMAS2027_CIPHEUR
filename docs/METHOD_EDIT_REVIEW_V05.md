## Scientific boundaries

The finite-catalogue minimum applies only when every master is solved globally and the final entire quotient is acyclic. It is not a minimum over unrestricted LLM proposals, the rule grammar, unseen graphs or measured shared-DAG runtime. Practical programme banks use each candidate's full declared-interface DAG gate and observed quality/work selector. Neither gate implies that the particular bounded scalar rule agrees with every certified requirement, and neither proves transfer.

The LLM's scientific role is an offline generator supplied with graph/boundary examples, schedule feedback, allowed operations and optional certified relations/equality joins. It proposes a typed structural interface and deterministic rule; independent tools perform verification, scoring and selection. Deployment invokes the frozen executable, without LLM or conditional-oracle calls. The text makes no model-specific quality advantage, natural-obstruction discovery or new pilot result claim.

The registered V05 pilot maximizes the same utility J in every arm and has no reached-regret tie-break. The historical V04 near-best/reached-regret selector and its zero observed primary-selection effect belong in the experiments/protocol reports. Any retained V04 outcomes must keep their original freeze/selector provenance; they must not be relabeled as newly selected by the V05 rule.
