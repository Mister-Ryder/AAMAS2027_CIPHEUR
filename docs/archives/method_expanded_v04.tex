\section{Certified Representation--Rule Refinement}
\label{sec:method}

Offline refinement tests input expressivity, action relevance at reached
states, and complete-schedule quality versus measured work
(Figure~\ref{fig:method}). Banks remain fixed during evaluation; the selected
feature--rule program is frozen before fresh confirmation and deployment.

\subsection{Component-Cancelled Full-Residual Certificates}
\label{sec:method:certificates}
At an admissible boundary $B=(F,X)$, write $R=R_\theta(B)$ and
$R_d=R\setminus(\{d\}\cup N_\theta(d))$. Let $\mathcal C_d$ be the
connected components of $G_\theta[R_d]$, identified by exact vertex sets,
and let $\alpha_w(C)$ denote the MWIS value of their induced graph.
Additivity gives
\begin{equation}
 V_\theta(d\mid B)=w(F)+w_d+\sum_{C\in\mathcal C_d}\alpha_w(C).
 \label{eq:component-completion}
\end{equation}
For actions $a,b$ in one graph and boundary, define unmatched
collections $\mathcal A=\mathcal C_a\setminus\mathcal C_b$ and
$\mathcal B=\mathcal C_b\setminus\mathcal C_a$. Shared components have
identical weighted induced graphs; their possibly unknown optima cancel:
\begin{equation}
 \begin{split}
 \Delta_{ab}&=V_\theta(a\mid B)-V_\theta(b\mid B)\\
 &=w_a-w_b+\sum_{C\in\mathcal A}\alpha_w(C)
             -\sum_{C\in\mathcal B}\alpha_w(C).
 \end{split}
 \label{eq:cancelled-difference}
\end{equation}
Given sound component intervals $[L_C,U_C]$,
\begin{equation}
 \begin{split}
 \underline\Delta_{ab}&=w_a-w_b+\sum_{\mathcal A}L_C-\sum_{\mathcal B}U_C,\\
 \overline\Delta_{ab}&=w_a-w_b+\sum_{\mathcal A}U_C-\sum_{\mathcal B}L_C.
 \end{split}
 \label{eq:difference-envelope}
\end{equation}
\paragraph{Lemma (sound cancellation).}
The endpoints enclose the full conditional-value difference.
\emph{Proof.} Independent component optima have a feasible union. Shared
terms cancel; bounding unmatched terms with their respective signs proves
the enclosure.\hfill$\square$
Conditioning can disconnect a connected graph. Matching requires exact
vertex sets within one configuration, not isomorphism or cross-intervention
identity (Figure~\ref{fig:cancellation}).

Checked feasible packings supply lower bounds. For $H\subseteq C$, a sound
$U_H$ and verified clique partition $\{K_j\}$ of $C\setminus H$ give
\begin{equation}
 U_C=U_H+\sum_j\max_{v\in K_j}w_v.
 \label{eq:clique-envelope}
\end{equation}
Relaxing cross-region edges proves soundness. Greedy completion and
branch-and-bound use exact supplied-weight arithmetic; exported endpoints
round outward. Early stopping retains enclosures, large components use
zero-search bounds, and exhausted calls retain $[0,w(C)]$. Matched
zero-search audits fix actions and boundary; differing heuristic clique
partitions preclude a universal interval-dominance claim.

For aligned interventions, replay $a,b$ feasibly at the same $B$ on both
sides. With $\epsilon\geq0$, certify reversal when
\begin{equation}
 \underline\Delta^{\theta_0}_{ab}>\epsilon,
 \qquad \overline\Delta^{\theta_1}_{ab}<-\epsilon,
 \label{eq:reversal-certificate}
\end{equation}
or with roles exchanged. Equal strict signs certify preservation;
unresolved intervals and ties remain unknown at budget exhaustion. Certified sides supply
occurrences $x=(G_\theta,R,v)$ and requirements
\begin{equation}
 h(\phi(x^+))>h(\phi(x^-)).
 \label{eq:strict-requirement}
\end{equation}
Objective tolerance $\epsilon$ differs from a scoring margin.

\subsection{Exact Quotients and Finite-Catalogue Repair}
\label{sec:method:repair}
For finite evidence, $Q_\phi$ identifies occurrences exactly when
$\phi(x)=\phi(y)$ and adds arcs $[x^+]\to[x^-]$, retaining self-loops.
The nine base inputs are weight, duration, residual degree, neighbor-weight
sum/maximum, compatible-weight sum, ground/satellite gaps and residual count.
Diagnosis includes every declared field, even if ignored by the rule;
IDs/split labels are excluded. A configuration audit adds omitted satellite
transition time and presence before attributing aliases to graph structure.

\paragraph{Proposition 1 (finite representability).}
An unrestricted deterministic pointwise scalar score satisfies every
observed strict requirement if and only if $Q_\phi$ is acyclic.
\emph{Proof.} Cycles demand a strict decrease back to the same score.
Longest outgoing-path lengths on a finite DAG satisfy every arc and scale
to any positive margin.\hfill$\square$

A concrete cycle records ranking arcs $p_i\to n_i$ and equality joins
$n_i\sim_\phi p_{i+1}$: distinctions that LLM features should recover.
For finite typed catalogue $\mathcal F$, let $d_{Wf}=1$ when $f$ separates
any join of witness $W$. With positive standalone costs $c_f$, solve
\begin{equation}
 \begin{split}
 \min_{z\in\{0,1\}^{|\mathcal F|}}\ &\sum_f c_fz_f,\\
 \text{s.t. }&\sum_f d_{Wf}z_f\geq1\quad\text{for each retained }W,\\
 &\sum_f z_f\leq K.
 \end{split}
 \label{eq:repair-cover}
\end{equation}
Trials augment the fixed base, replacing previous trial features, and
rebuild the full quotient. Surviving cycles add necessary cuts: splitting
a coarse cycle can leave a refined cycle projecting to a repeated-class
closed walk.

\paragraph{Proposition 2 (finite-catalogue repair).}
Globally solved masters followed by a final acyclic full quotient minimize
the stated additive cost over the fixed catalogue, evidence and limit $K$.
\emph{Proof.} Necessary witness constraints make the master optimum a
lower bound on valid repair cost. The final DAG attains that bound.
\hfill$\square$

With variable $K$, arbitrary finite tables encode weighted set cover:
one self-loop pair per element, separated by covering-subset features.
This does not establish hardness for every graph grammar; fixed $K$
permits $O(|\mathcal F|^K)$ trials. Exhaustion or uncovered witnesses yield
unresolved repair. Acyclicity implies finite representability, not that a
bounded rule fits or transfers. Minimum-interface search remains an ablation.

\subsection{Auditing Actual Reached Actions}
\label{sec:method:actions}
Requirements can concern unchosen actions. Complete rollouts record the
initial boundary and up to two commitments. Prespecified audits retain
the initial and most-shared boundary, with deterministic ties and
unsampled-state counts.

Replay each program's deterministic argmax at the same $B$, forming finite
pool $\mathcal U_B$. Query all unordered pairs in fixed order under shared
cache/budget. For chosen $d$, define
\begin{equation}
 \rho_{\mathcal U_B}(d)=
 \max_{u\in\mathcal U_B}\bigl[V_\theta(u\mid B)-V_\theta(d\mid B)\bigr].
 \label{eq:pool-regret}
\end{equation}
Since $d\in\mathcal U_B$, maxima of zero and difference endpoints enclose
$\rho$. The upper endpoint bounds only finite-pool regret; the lower can
prove loss to a particular feasible alternative. Neither guarantees
complete-schedule loss. Counterfactual states remain auditable, but
selection uses only boundaries in that program's recorded rollout.
Coverage, failed choices and unknown comparisons accompany regret;
strict differences augment quotient evidence.

\subsection{Typed Features and Demand-Driven Execution}
\label{sec:method:features}
The typed language distinguishes nodes, node sets, edge sets and numbers;
proposals pass type, size and finite-output checks. Neighborhood/set
operations, induced edges and bounded arithmetic compose packing/cover, edge-count
and edge-minimum features, including continuation summaries of $R_v$.
For example,
\begin{equation}
 T_R(v)=\sum_{\{u,z\}\in E_\theta[N_R(v)]}\min(w_u,w_z)
 \label{eq:neighbor-structure}
\end{equation}
repeatedly counts shared endpoints, making it a statistic, not a completion
bound. Rounded packing/cover scorer inputs are heuristic; the independent
sound offline bound oracle uses exact arithmetic to certify labels (Figure~\ref{fig:repair}).

State-wide guards $|R|\leq64$ or $128$ choose
$w_v+(L_v+U_v)/2$ using rounded packing/cover summaries of $R_v$,
otherwise density/pressure scoring.
Every action shares the branch; midpoints give no ranking guarantee.

Shared DAGs and lazy conditional/short-circuit inputs avoid untaken generic
primitives; static aggregate-superset initialization/maintenance stays charged.
For defined finite eager features, scores, ties and complete traces are
preserved. Diagnosis retains the full interface. Sequential deletion uses
\begin{equation}
 T(v)\leftarrow T(v)-\sum_{y\in N_R(v)\cap N_R(x)}\min(w_x,w_y).
 \label{eq:incremental-feature}
\end{equation}
Updated neighborhoods prevent double subtraction; exact accumulators
preserve rounded reference terms. Unsupported expressions use the snapshot
interpreter. All compilation, cache, update and scoring work is charged;
joint DAG cost is nonadditive, unlike $c_f$.

A separate backend caches only reviewed root/neighborhood dependencies
under immutable graph, weights, configuration and program. After deleting
the whole batch $D$, it refreshes survivors in the pre-deletion frontier
$\bigcup_{x\in D}N_R(x)$;
outside this frontier, unchanged inputs preserve scores. Versioned heap
entries preserve scalar-score/string-ID ordering. Global or unreviewed
demanded inputs, even in untaken branches, trigger full rescoring.
Initialization, frontier refreshes, comparisons and stale removal are
charged. For valid queried inputs this preserves the score-sliced
kernel's trace and frozen feature--rule pair.

\subsection{TRAIN Selection and Frozen Deployment}
\label{sec:method:selection}
One offline assistant session authors proposals before TRAIN outcomes.
Controls have unequal authoring counts/compute. Numerical selection uses
TRAIN only, excluding fresh validation/test outcomes.

For context $t$, $q_{Pt}$ divides reward by the best completed bank reward,
a feasible empirical comparator; $c_{Pt}$ divides measured work by positive
degree-baseline work. Incomplete runs receive $q_{Pt}=0,c_{Pt}=100$.
Equal-family means define $q(P),c(P)$ and
\begin{equation}
 J(P)=q(P)-0.002\,[c(P)-1].
 \label{eq:joint-utility}
\end{equation}
New guided/free/enumerated arms require acyclic declared-interface quotients;
prior guided/rule-only/baseline controls retain separate scope. Within each
arm, programs within 0.002 of its highest utility rank by reached
pool-regret upper endpoint,
then lower endpoint, unknown count, utility and fixed order. Regret uses
full-context normalization $\max(1,\operatorname{mean}_{v\in V}w_v)$ and
equal-family means. Missing coverage sorts last; unknowns are not exclusion
gates. Primary-only guided selection remains an ablation.

All programs use the common demanded backend and cooperative five
process-CPU-second target;
overshoot/completion are recorded. Failure returns no schedule or fallback.
Bank, evidence, program and compiler/configuration hashes freeze before
fresh confirmation. Online commitment deletes the highest-score action's
closed neighborhood. The fixed kernel guarantees feasibility;
quality/transfer remain empirical.
