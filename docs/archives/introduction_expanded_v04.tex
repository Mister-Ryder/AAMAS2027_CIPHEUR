\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Selecting a satellite--ground contact commits scarce resources and removes
competing opportunities. The reward forgone depends on whether those
alternatives could coexist. Switching intervals and satellite constraints
change this relationship while contact windows and rewards remain fixed.
Operational scheduling already models coupled resources~\cite{augenstein2016optimal,
levinson2025optimal}. We study pairwise-conflict scheduling, where a reusable
maximum-weight independent set (MWIS) heuristic must respond to different
static configurations.

Large language models (LLMs) synthesize executable heuristics through
objective feedback in FunSearch, EoH and ReEvo~\cite{romeraparedes2024funsearch,
liu2024eoh,ye2024reevo}; DRAGON employs decomposition and reconstruction
agents with boundary constraints and checker feedback~\cite{chen2026dragon}.
Our question concerns the scoring interface: how can synthesis identify
missing information that makes required rankings impossible? Formula
revision cannot satisfy incompatible strict preferences over identical
observable inputs.

Figure~\ref{fig:motivation} shows a constructed alias with certified preferences. Contacts
$a,b$ each have reward $8$ and three conflicting alternatives of reward $6$,
whose internal conflicts differ. Their conditional completion values,
excluding the common fixed reward, are $20,26$ before a ground-gap
intervention and $20,14$ afterward. The preference reverses, yet both
actions remain individually feasible and share the complete base feature
vector within each configuration. Every pointwise scorer of that vector
ties them. Shared configuration fields cannot separate them; a neighborhood
feature can. These values concern forced completions, rather than an
unrestricted rollout's reward.

We propose certified representation--rule refinement. Following established
abstraction-refinement and planning-feature precedents~\cite{clarke2000cegar,
frances2021policies}, it projects sound strict preferences onto exact
feature-equivalence classes. Directed cycles certify an information
obstruction. Concrete witnesses retain the ranking arcs and equality joins
closing each cycle, directing LLM proposals toward distinguishing graph
operations. A feature master repeatedly checks the full refined quotient,
because separating an initial witness can leave other cycles intact.

Supporting mechanisms connect this diagnosis to scheduling decisions.
Within one graph, components shared by the two conditioned residuals cancel
from their conditional-value difference, avoiding bounds on shared unknown optima.
Audits compare actual argmax actions at reached rollout boundaries; their
regret envelopes concern a finite action pool and retain unresolved queries.
Demand-driven execution shares typed computations and charges compiled
work. TRAIN selection combines complete-schedule reward and cost, using
reached-action regret within a declared utility band.

Our contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Certified information diagnosis:} a quotient characterization
of finite pointwise representability, with concrete alias and cycle witnesses.
\item \textbf{Witness-guided structural repair:} full-quotient separation
and minimum additive-cost refinement over fixed evidence, a finite feature
catalogue and a declared feature limit.
\item \textbf{Decision-aware joint synthesis:} component-cancelled evidence,
reached-action audits and demanded computation support executable
feature--rule selection beyond relational consistency alone.
\end{enumerate}

One authoring session supplies 12 new proposals alongside existing
24-proposal banks; generation counts and compute are not matched.
Numerical selection uses TRAIN only. Frozen confirmation covers fresh
scheduling instances and transfer to public graphs against published MWIS
implementations~\cite{lamm2019weighted,gellner2021struction,
grossmann2024mmwis,grossmann2025chils}. A fixed constructive kernel enforces
graph feasibility without online LLM or oracle calls. Schedule quality
and transfer remain empirical questions.
