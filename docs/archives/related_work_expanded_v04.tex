\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Satellite scheduling and learned graph decisions.}
Constellation planning couples imaging, downlinks, and operational resources:
mixed-integer decomposition~\cite{augenstein2016optimal}, integrated
collection/downlink planning~\cite{levinson2025optimal}, and ground-queue-aware
traffic scheduling~\cite{tao2023transmitting} already establish the importance
of resource context. Our pairwise-conflict model is narrower; it does not
represent arbitrary cumulative resource constraints. Graph embeddings can
condition optimization decisions on partial solutions~\cite{khalil2017learning},
and learned deferral changes independent-set decision stages~\cite{ahn2020defer}.
AAMAS methods combine factor-graph inference with routing~\cite{li2024factor}
and enrich neural representations with local topology~\cite{bai2025local}.
Structural inputs are therefore established design choices.

Specialized MWIS algorithms provide stronger optimization comparators:
weighted branch-and-reduce~\cite{lamm2019weighted}, increasing
transformations~\cite{gellner2021struction}, memetic
kernelization~\cite{grossmann2024mmwis}, and concurrent iterated local
search~\cite{grossmann2025chils}. We evaluate their published implementations
on the same graphs, measuring reward and end-to-end time separately from
the frozen scoring program's structural diagnosis.

GNN expressiveness analyses identify indistinguishability under aggregation
architectures~\cite{xu2019powerful,morris2019weisfeiler}. Our obstruction instead
uses exact equality of the complete declared feature vector: a strict-ranking
cycle proves inexpressibility for every pointwise scalar function on the
observed occurrences. This is neither a graph-isomorphism test nor a lower
bound on all graph policies. The supplied AAMAS graph-planning theory paper
also uses structural witnesses~\cite{dhar2026universal}, but studies universal
motion reachability rather than heuristic representation repair.

\paragraph{LLM algorithm discovery.}
FunSearch searches priority functions inside fixed skeletons~\cite{romeraparedes2024funsearch};
EoH evolves thoughts and code~\cite{liu2024eoh}, and ReEvo guides revisions by
reflection~\cite{ye2024reevo}. EoH-S optimizes complementary heuristic
sets~\cite{liu2026eohs}; LACE combines a verified problem interface, tool library,
and runtime-constrained portfolio evolution~\cite{gong2026lace}.
Offline discovery, diverse heuristics, and cost budgets are precedents.
DRAGON uses per-instance decomposition and reconstruction agents, transmitting
boundary constraints and checker feedback~\cite{chen2026dragon}.
Our distinguishing mechanism diagnoses whether the scoring interface itself
can express certified conditional rankings, then targets its missing
structural distinctions. Zero online model calls alone establish no advantage.

\paragraph{Abstraction and program synthesis.}
Counterexample-guided abstraction refinement uses failure witnesses to refine
an abstraction~\cite{clarke2000cegar}. Generalized planning learns symbolic
features and actions~\cite{bonet2019features}, including joint feature selection
and good/bad-transition separation~\cite{frances2021policies}.
DreamCoder learns reusable program components with synthesis guidance~\cite{ellis2021dreamcoder}.
Our scheduling contract couples aligned resource interventions and feasible
boundary replay to certified rankings, equality-join feature selection, and
repeated full-quotient separation. It diagnoses an information obstruction
rather than adding features solely after low reward. Acyclicity removes that
observed obstruction; bounded-language realizability, shared computation cost,
and complete-schedule quality remain separate requirements.
