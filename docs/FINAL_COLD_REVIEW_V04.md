## Findings at the original review snapshot

1. **Clarify what “specification consistency” means in the abstract.** `paper/main.tex:27` says feature–rule pairs are selected jointly for specification consistency. A reader can reasonably infer that the selected numerical rule satisfies all certified orders. The implemented gate instead tests acyclicity of the whole declared feature interface. The frozen primary has 631 strict agreements and 193 violations on the 824 saved TRAIN requirements. Suggested replacement: “Feature–rule pairs pass a declared-interface acyclicity gate and are selected using complete-schedule quality, measured cost and reached finite-pool regret.” This is a wording correction, not a new gate or selection. Root owns the abstract, which this reviewer did not edit.

2. **Use sound-bound terminology for the budgeted oracle.** `paper/sections/method.tex:184` calls the independent certifier an “exact offline oracle.” Its arithmetic is exact and its bounds are sound, but a budgeted MWIS call need not return an exact optimum. Prefer “sound offline bound oracle using exact arithmetic.” At line 71, “exhausted budgets remain unknown” is too broad for the component-difference engine: a sound retained interval can have a strict sign even after search or call exhaustion. Prefer “Unresolved bounds and ties remain unknown at budget exhaustion.” The source emits a strict label only from the exact lower/upper sign test, including when components receive sound zero-search or trivial intervals. Neither issue invalidates the cancellation proof; both should prevent a stronger operational interpretation than the implementation supports.

3. **Keep the main empirical story comparative and separate from representability.** The strongest remaining presentation need is the completed fresh/public comparison with coverage and actual cost, alongside the new TRAIN mechanism figure. Interface acyclicity, scalar agreement and scheduling reward are different quantities; the primary does not fit every label, and its reached regret interval is not an all-action guarantee. Published solvers' 100% competitive reward is relative to the strongest saved feasible witness, not a proof of optimality. The current fresh prose explicitly disclaims published-solver superiority. Complete the public family comparison before drawing a public conclusion; keep the separately prescribed long subset and later heap extension distinct. This is an evidence-presentation priority, not an independently discovered numerical error.

## Mathematical and implementation contract

| Mechanism | Checked interpretation | Limit that must remain explicit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact quotient | Equality uses the entire declared finite input vector; strict arcs include self-loops. A finite DAG admits an unrestricted deterministic pointwise scalar score, and a cycle forbids one. | The topological construction does not prove fit by the bounded AST, unseen consistency or schedule quality. | Concrete repair | A cycle retains ranking endpoints and exact equality joins. Every valid refinement separates at least one join, making each master cut necessary. A final DAG attains the accumulated master lower bound. | Optimum is over fixed evidence, a finite catalogue, positive additive standalone costs and the declared limit K. Their unknown optimal values cancel; signed component intervals enclose the remaining difference. | Requires additive MWIS utility and model-complete pairwise conflicts.
