## Overall scientific assessment

The clearest contribution is **a certified, finite obstruction to the current pointwise input interface, with concrete equality joins that direct representation repair**. It is narrower and more defensible than a new LLM optimizer, generic feature invention, or stronger local search. Keeping the complete cross-state quotient, including non-self-loop cycles, makes the method more substantive than detecting two equal vectors with opposite labels. The fixed-catalogue result supplies an independently checked optimization consequence of that diagnosis.

The current evidence supports a partial learning result, not the full scheduling result. W produces more eligible interfaces than R in the declared four-block cohort and in the descriptive five-block inventory. R nevertheless has stronger overall numerical fit and lower selected work; every candidate has identical TRAIN schedule quality. The exact catalogue repairs representability, but supplies neither a fitted rule nor transfer. This is a useful, honest finding: a representable interface can still yield a poor rule, and a better full-snapshot rule need not improve local search.

The theoretical arguments are correct under the stated assumptions, but are not independently deep new optimization theory: Theorem 1 is a finite strict-order/topological-ranking characterization; Theorem 2 is an exact separation/cut-generation argument with a fixed catalogue. The paper's depth must come from the combination of sound optimization-derived targets, an exact information diagnosis, a real typed authoring process, and evidence identifying which links do or do not work. Adding mature solver components cannot supply the missing learning evidence.

## P1: exact-equality scope is a major empirical limitation

The quotient identifies exact equality of the complete represented input. That is a genuine impossibility certificate, but its prevalence depends strongly on the input distribution. Here reward and duration are both one. Consequently, several base-nine fields collapse to degree-related summaries: neighbor-weight sum equals degree, compatible-weight sum follows residual count and degree, and neighbor maximum is largely constant. Equal vectors are therefore much easier to obtain than on heterogeneous weighted contacts.

Conversely, if each observed reward is distinct and reward is included in the vector, finite occurrence vectors can become unique even when important neighborhood information is absent. The quotient may then be acyclic trivially: an unrestricted lookup scalar could satisfy the finite labels, although the bounded grammar cannot fit or generalize them. Theorem 1 remains valid, but it does not detect that latter limitation. Do not equate a DAG with broadly adequate representation, a structurally useful feature set, or sufficiency for the chosen grammar. Approximate equality and near-collision robustness are different problems; the current exact theorem does not cover them.

Actionable presentation: keep “finite observed pointwise representability” near the first theorem and the main result table; explicitly connect the enriched unit-weight design to its diagnostic purpose. Report the predeclared withheld strata separately, including exact alias/cycle incidence where the frozen protocol already supplies it. The fresh weighted performance inventory tests deployment utility, not natural incidence of certified information obstructions. Do not silently add new TEST label acquisition or use bridge/evaluation findings as authoring feedback.

ID exclusion is also narrower than invariance: ID-based tie orders inside greedy packing/clique operations can create a distinction that changes after renaming. The catalogue theorem certifies the original feature table, not an ID-invariant physical mechanism. Preserve the registered renaming assay, and distinguish rule-fit/feature changes from the common kernel's own tie-order variation. A successful original join split is not by itself a robust structural explanation.

## Relative contribution to the supplied DRAGON paper

The supplied paper is DRAGON, AAMAS 2026, pp. 2809–2817, DOI 10.65109/SBAY6258. The retained PDF review documents its online decomposer/reconstructor, compressed static region, boundary constraints, feasibility-check feedback, memory of failed attempts and acceptance mechanism. It already demonstrates context/boundary-aware LLM cooperation. Our novelty cannot be “first to use boundaries,” “first to generate context-sensitive optimization decisions,” or “LLM plus local classical repair.”

Our defensible difference is a reusable offline priority program whose input interface is diagnosed against sound full-residual conditional inequalities, with exact quotient conflicts and explicit equality joins. That is a different scientific question and a more formal specification mechanism. It is not a demonstrated strict upgrade over DRAGON: problem classes, online/offline costs, deployment objects and current empirical strength differ. DRAGON's multi-domain quality/cost study is more mature than the current completed V06 deployment evidence. Its own LLM-decomposition/solver-reconstruction ablation is a reminder that strong classical reconstruction can account for quality gains.

The current related-work sentence disclaiming a demonstrated DRAGON advantage is appropriate. Keep it. A comparison of deployment cost would require accounting for offline discovery, online instance count, native initialization and actual timing; eliminating online LLM calls is a design fact, not by itself proof of lower amortized cost or better quality. There is no need to invent agent-theoretic novelty merely to fit AAMAS: the SOPS/hybrid classical-generative contribution should stand on the evidence above.

The closest *synthesis* comparisons are still EoH/ReEvo/LLaMEA and the newer complete-program or derivation-memory systems already cited. Open-ended code can invent structural computations. What is new here must remain the optimization-derived obstruction and its targeted use, not joint code/feature editing as such. Do not claim universal priority over all representation-learning or abstraction-refinement literature.
