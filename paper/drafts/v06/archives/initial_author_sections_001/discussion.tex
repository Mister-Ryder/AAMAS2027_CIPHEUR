\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Certified equality joins identify information failures that rule tuning alone
cannot repair. The DAG converse permits an unrestricted real-valued scalar
only on finite observed vectors; it ensures neither bounded-grammar
expressibility nor transfer. Numerical fit remains separate, and
full-residual certificates do not establish optimal greedy proposals or pivots
inside restricted patches. Feasibility and incumbent preservation come from
the shared classical kernel~\cite{lamm2019weighted}.

The alias-enriched pilot deliberately stresses diagnosis. Unit-reward,
unit-duration contacts and 32-vertex source-induced graphs cannot establish
natural physical-incidence prevalence or large weighted-instance effectiveness.
Weight distributions can affect algorithmic behavior~\cite{mccreesh2017evaluation}.
Public source clusters are split within a previously exposed corpus: new
states and labels are withheld, but the corpus is not unseen.

The authoring contrast conditions on transport-complete blocks. Failed
requests remain in unconditional accounting; transport success is not assumed
missing at random. This conditional cohort cannot estimate unconditional
system efficacy. Matching requested settings and proposal slots does not match
token use or latency, and a few authoring blocks cannot establish a
model-population advantage.

ID-based ties inside packing and clique primitives can change features under
relabeling despite excluding explicit ID lookup. Polynomial features can still
dominate a short repair budget, so their measured work belongs in selection;
acyclicity alone says nothing about computational affordability. Cooperative nominal wall
targets allow guard costs and overshoot; equal targets need not mean equal
total computation. Charged operation work and the catalogue's additive optimum
are distinct, and neither proves minimum deployment runtime.
