\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

A satellite--ground contact occupies shared resources and excludes competing
opportunities. The reward forgone by accepting it depends on which alternatives
can coexist. Changing a switching threshold can therefore change a useful
ranking while preserving every contact's reward and time window. Operational
scheduling couples collection, storage and communication resources~\cite{augenstein2016optimal,levinson2025optimal}.
We study the pairwise-conflict setting, formulated as maximum-weight independent
set (MWIS), and ask how a reusable heuristic can adapt to different static
resource configurations.

LLM-based algorithm synthesis already generates executable heuristics through
evaluation and selection. FunSearch searches programs, EoH evolves descriptions
and code, and ReEvo adds reflective feedback~\cite{romeraparedes2024funsearch,liu2024eoh,ye2024reevo}.
DRAGON instead invokes decomposition and reconstruction agents while solving
an instance, carrying local boundary constraints~\cite{chen2026dragon}.
These approaches can introduce structural computations; our question concerns
the evidence directing that choice. Aggregate reward does not identify whether
a failed decision requires a different formula or information absent from its
inputs. Repeated rule revision cannot satisfy incompatible strict preferences
over identical observations.

Figure~\ref{fig:motivation} makes this limitation concrete. Competing actions
$a,b$ have equal rewards and identical nine-input base vectors within each
configuration. Their forced-completion increments are $20,26$ before a
ground-gap intervention and $20,14$ afterward. Both actions remain individually
feasible, but the preferred action changes from $b$ to $a$. Every deterministic
pointwise scalar using the base vectors ties them on each side. Neighborhood
compatibility supplies a distinction that their marginal summaries omit.
The increments exclude the same fixed boundary reward; they are conditional
completion values, not unrestricted rollout returns or evidence of successful
LLM synthesis.

We propose certified representation--rule refinement. Controlled offline
interventions hold contacts, rewards and explicit fixed/excluded commitments
constant while changing an admissible resource parameter. Sound bounds on
full-residual conditional completion differences produce strict reversal or
preservation requirements; unresolved comparisons abstain. Identical residual
components cancel within each configuration, avoiding unnecessary optimization
without ignoring external opportunity costs. The resulting inequalities specify
which rankings should change and which should remain stable.

The diagnosis identifies occurrences with exactly equal represented features
and projects every strict requirement onto the complete feature quotient.
An unrestricted deterministic pointwise scalar satisfies the finite requirements
if and only if this directed quotient is acyclic. A cycle retains its concrete
ranking arcs and equality joins, exposing occurrences that must become
distinguishable. Following representation and abstraction refinement
precedents~\cite{clarke2000cegar,frances2021policies}, these joins guide offline
LLM composition of typed structural features together with a ranking rule.
Every proposed interface rebuilds the full quotient: splitting one witness
need not remove another cycle. Acyclicity establishes finite representability,
not bounded-grammar fit or unseen-instance correctness. An optional exact
finite-catalogue repair minimizes additive feature cost under its declared
scope; practical synthesis separately measures shared feature-computation work.

Selection distinguishes the demanded interface, actual scalar fit and complete
schedule quality. Unused feature declarations cannot repair the demanded
quotient. TRAIN-only selection ranks eligible feature--rule pairs by strict
fit, scheduling reward and charged work before freezing them. All authoring
arms share a Degree initializer and bounded multi-vertex repair using classical
clique bounds and branch-and-bound~\cite{lamm2019weighted,grossmann2025chils}.
The program orders local feasible greedy additions and pivot vertices; it
does not change feasibility or pruning. This mature solver component is
shared infrastructure. Deployment retains a feasible incumbent without online
LLM or full-residual certification calls, while still performing bounded
classical optimization.

Our contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Information diagnosis:} an exact finite representability
characterization with concrete cycle witnesses, separating insufficient
inputs from a poorly fitted rule.
\item \textbf{Targeted, cost-aware composition:} certified full-residual
requirements and explicit equality joins direct joint typed feature--rule
repair, with complete quotient checking and a scoped additive-cost theorem.
\item \textbf{Frozen-program evaluation:} joint specification, reward and
work assessment isolates authoring information under a common repair kernel
and tests adaptation after TRAIN-only selection.
\end{enumerate}

Independent TRAIN evidence contains strict equal-vector conflicts in
source-derived public graphs and controlled contact states. Our empirical
study compares witness-guided, relations-only and objective-only cold
authoring and a separately registered refinement of a common rejected seed.
