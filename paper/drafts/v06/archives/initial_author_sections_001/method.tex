\section{Certified Representation--Rule Refinement}
\label{sec:method}

The framework separates three questions: whether the inputs can express
certified action preferences, whether a proposed scalar obeys them, and
whether its deployment improves a feasible schedule within budget. Offline
certification exposes information obstructions; typed synthesis proposes
distinctions and rules; a shared bounded repair kernel evaluates their
algorithmic consequences. None of these checks substitutes for the others.

\subsection{Sound Full-Residual Decision Evidence}
\label{sec:method:certificates}
At admissible boundary $B=(F,X)$, let $A=R_\theta(B)$ and
$A_d=A\setminus(\{d\}\cup N_\theta(d))$. Write $\mathcal C_d$ for
the connected components of $G_\theta[A_d]$, identified by their exact
vertex sets. Component independence yields
\begin{equation}
 V_\theta(d\mid B)=w(F)+w_d+\sum_{C\in\mathcal C_d}\alpha_w(G_\theta[C]),
 \label{eq:component-completion}
\end{equation}
where $\alpha_w$ is the MWIS value. For actions $a,b$ in the same graph
and boundary, define $\mathcal A=\mathcal C_a\setminus\mathcal C_b$
and $\mathcal B=\mathcal C_b\setminus\mathcal C_a$. Shared terms cancel:
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_{ab}=w_a-w_b+\sum_{C\in\mathcal A}\alpha_w(G_\theta[C])
                         -\sum_{C\in\mathcal B}\alpha_w(G_\theta[C]).
 \label{eq:cancelled-difference}
\end{equation}
Given sound component intervals $[L_C,U_C]$, set
\begin{equation}
 \begin{split}
 \underline\Delta_{ab}&=w_a-w_b+\sum_{\mathcal A}L_C-\sum_{\mathcal B}U_C,\\
 \overline\Delta_{ab}&=w_a-w_b+\sum_{\mathcal A}U_C-\sum_{\mathcal B}L_C.
 \end{split}
 \label{eq:difference-envelope}
\end{equation}
\paragraph{Lemma 1 (within-graph cancellation).}
These endpoints enclose the full conditional-completion difference.
\emph{Proof.} Component optima unite feasibly; identical induced components
have identical values and cancel. Applying lower or upper bounds according
to each remaining term's sign proves the enclosure.\hfill$\square$

Equal vertex names across configurations do not justify cancellation:
their induced edges can differ. Each configuration is certified separately.
Equal component sizes or total weights are also insufficient. Matching uses
the complete residual vertex set within its source graph; partly overlapping
components retain their separate bounds. Cancelling a shared unknown optimum
does not require that optimum to have been solved.
Verified independent packings supply lower bounds. For $H\subseteq C$,
a sound bound $U_H$ and a checked clique partition $\{K_j\}$ of $C\setminus H$
give
\begin{equation}
 U_C=U_H+\sum_j\max_{v\in K_j}w_v.
 \label{eq:clique-envelope}
\end{equation}
Ignoring cross-region edges relaxes feasibility. Intersecting repeated valid
intervals uses the largest feasible lower bound and smallest sound upper
bound; distinct partitions need not dominate. Exact rational arithmetic and
outward display rounding preserve certificates. Unsearched components retain
$[0,w(C)]$, so exhausted budgets remain unknown.

Aligned interventions preserve contacts, weights and the replayed boundary,
with both competing actions feasible on both sides. For objective tolerance
$\epsilon\geq0$, $\underline\Delta^{\theta_0}_{ab}>\epsilon$ and
$\overline\Delta^{\theta_1}_{ab}<-\epsilon$ certify a reversal; equal strict
signs certify preservation. Other intervals supply no strict label. Each
certified occurrence pair requires
\begin{equation}
 h(\phi(x^+))>h(\phi(x^-)),\qquad x=(G_\theta,A,v).
 \label{eq:strict-requirement}
\end{equation}
Label provenance binds the graph, fixed/excluded sets, actions and signed
intervals. Unresolved attempts remain in the acquisition record rather than
being counted as preservation or constraints satisfied by a candidate.

\subsection{Information Obstructions and Complete Quotients}
\label{sec:method:repair}
For finite observed requirements, identify occurrences with exactly equal
represented feature values. The quotient $Q_\phi$ has one vertex per class
and an arc $[x^+]\to[x^-]$ for every strict requirement, retaining self-loops.
Its base interface comprises reward, duration, degree, neighbor-weight
sum/maximum, compatible-weight sum, ground/satellite gaps and residual count.
IDs and dataset labels are excluded. Omitted configuration fields must be
audited before attributing an alias specifically to neighborhood structure.

\paragraph{Theorem 1 (finite pointwise representability).}
An unrestricted deterministic pointwise scalar satisfies all observed
requirements if and only if $Q_\phi$ is acyclic.
\emph{Proof.} A directed cycle demands a strict score decrease back to the
same feature vector, which is impossible. On a finite DAG, assigning each
class its longest outgoing-path length strictly decreases along every arc.
Scaling these finite values gives any positive margin.\hfill$\square$

A cycle therefore rules out every scalar using that interface, rather than
just the current formula. The converse establishes existence on the finite
observed vectors; it does not establish expressibility in the bounded rule
grammar, perfect fit by the proposed rule, or behavior on unseen graphs.
The witness records concrete arcs $p_i\to n_i$ and equality joins
$n_i\sim_\phi p_{i+1}$, with cyclic indices. These endpoint pairs explain
which indistinguishable occurrences a new feature must separate.
Joins may connect different configurations or boundaries. Consequently,
acyclicity of each state's quotient alone cannot establish acyclicity of
their combined requirements. Diagnosis always retains the complete observed
occurrence inventory.

\subsection{Scoped Minimum-Cost Interface Repair}
Let $\mathcal F$ be a fixed finite catalogue evaluated at every occurrence,
with positive standalone costs $c_f$ and at most $K$ additions. Set
$d_{Wf}=1$ exactly when $f$ separates at least one concrete equality join
of witness $W$. The optional repair master is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{split}
 \min_{z\in\{0,1\}^{|\mathcal F|}}\quad&\sum_f c_fz_f,\\
 \text{subject to}\quad&\sum_f d_{Wf}z_f\geq1\quad(\text{retained }W),\\
 &\sum_fz_f\leq K.
 \end{split}
 \label{eq:repair-cover}
\end{equation}
After every globally solved master, rebuild the \emph{entire} quotient using
the base and its selected additions. A surviving cycle supplies another cut.
Trials replace previous additions; base inputs remain fixed. A refined cycle
can project to a walk revisiting coarse classes, so checking only the original
cycle or its locally split joins is insufficient.

\paragraph{Theorem 2 (finite-catalogue additive optimum).}
If exact masters terminate with an acyclic full quotient, the selected
subset minimizes additive cost among catalogue repairs satisfying $K$.
\emph{Proof.} Every acyclic repair must split a join of each concrete
witness, hence satisfies every retained cut. Master cost lower-bounds every
valid repair. The final DAG is itself valid and attains that bound. Each
new cut excludes the current cyclic subset; finitely many subsets ensure
termination or infeasibility when search is unrestricted.\hfill$\square$

For arbitrary finite feature-value tables, element self-loops and covering
features encode weighted set cover. Fixed $K$ permits $O(|\mathcal F|^K)$
subset trials; the variable-$K$ reduction does not establish hardness for
every restricted typed library. Truncated masters and inseparable witnesses
remain unresolved. This theorem concerns fixed evidence, catalogue and
additive standalone cost, not globally optimal features, runtime or rules.
Practical proposal banks use full-quotient gates separately; they do not
inherit the master's optimality guarantee.

\subsection{Typed Proposals and Demanded Interfaces}
\label{sec:method:features}
An offline LLM proposes $P=(\phi_P,h_P)$ from a finite typed graph-operation
library and bounded numeric grammar. Operations compose neighbor and residual
sets, induced edges, weight aggregations, clique-cover summaries and feasible
greedy packings. These structural summaries are polynomial scorer inputs,
not conditional optima. Types, expression sizes and finite outputs are checked
before deployment; benchmark IDs and oracle/model calls are unavailable.
For example, aggregating minimum endpoint weights over edges induced among
neighbors can reveal compatibility structure absent from their marginal
weight totals. Its value must separate an actual witness join; merely
declaring a plausible structural summary is insufficient.

Refinement can reuse a rejected program: a complete quotient check supplies
concrete cycle arcs and equality joins at their original certified snapshots.
The next bounded proposal round receives that program and the missing
distinctions. Features may be replaced together with the rule, and the
entire retained requirement inventory is checked again. Repairing a local
witness therefore cannot silently discard a conflict elsewhere. Iterative
feedback uses TRAIN evidence only and stops at the declared authoring budget.

Matched authoring conditions share graph examples and common Degree repair
feedback. Relations receives certified inequalities; witness additionally
receives concrete equality joins; objective-only receives neither. Each
original proposal position remains recorded, including missing, invalid and
duplicate candidates. All conditions subsequently face the same controller
evaluation, including certified selection for objective-only proposals.

The gate retains all nine base coordinates and only additional features
syntactically referenced by the rule. Unused additions cannot repair it;
the complete declared interface is audited separately. Full-snapshot
evaluation checks declared features' finiteness at certified occurrences and
computes numerical rule fit on the original boundaries. Thus the demanded-coordinate gate
does not silently exempt unused declarations from safety checks. At execution,
shared expression DAGs and branch-sensitive input evaluation avoid redundant
feature computation. The program is evaluated on each immutable patch
snapshot; its resulting priority order guides the local greedy proposal and
search pivots. Initialization and all feature queries remain charged.

\subsection{Shared Bounded Repair Kernel}
\label{sec:method:execution}
Within each deployment track, all arms share the initializer and the same
bounded local-search component, following classical
MWIS optimization principles~\cite{lamm2019weighted,gellner2021struction,grossmann2024mmwis,grossmann2025chils}.
The default track uses exact weight/remaining-degree greedy initialization.
A second track supplies a common CHILS incumbent and charges its acquisition
to every complete pipeline. The kernel validates and retains this incumbent,
greedily fills any remaining compatible vertices, and applies the same repair.
For independent incumbent $I$, destroy movable $D\subseteq I\setminus F$ and set
\begin{equation}
 F_{\rm out}=I\setminus D,\qquad
 R=V\setminus\bigl(F_{\rm out}\cup N(F_{\rm out})\cup X\bigr),
 \label{eq:repair-region}
\end{equation}
then restrict to $D\subseteq R'\subseteq R$ when necessary. Starting from
feasible $D$, capped clique-bound branch-and-bound retains its best independent
$J\subseteq R'$. Since $J$ has no edge into $F_{\rm out}$, their union is
globally feasible; commit only a checked exact positive gain. Pruning certifies
only this restricted patch. Common Degree priorities choose targets, expansion
and restriction; the programme changes the local greedy proposal and pivot
vertex, while include-first traversal remains shared. Caps retain the feasible
incumbent, and errors remain explicit. Parsing, initialization, scoring, bounds,
search and validation consume the declared budget, with actual overshoot
reported. No online model or full-residual certification oracle is called.

\subsection{Joint TRAIN Selection and Freeze}
\label{sec:method:selection}
The controller evaluates all retained strict TRAIN requirements, beyond the
prescribed authoring examples. Eligibility requires a finite validated
programme, an acyclic demanded quotient and feasible, error-free kernel
execution on every TRAIN state. Normal anytime budget termination remains
eligible; invalid slots and empty eligible banks receive no replacement.

Let $m(P)$ count $h_P(\phi_P(x^+))>h_P(\phi_P(x^-))+\eta$, with the declared
score margin $\eta$. For family $t$ containing states $\mathcal D_t$, define
\begin{equation}
 q(P)=\frac1{|\mathcal T|}\sum_{t\in\mathcal T}\frac1{|\mathcal D_t|}
      \sum_{G\in\mathcal D_t}\frac{w(I_P(G))}{w(V_G)}.
 \label{eq:train-macro-quality}
\end{equation}
Zero-total-weight states use ratio one. This denominator is total input
reward, not an optimum or best-method reference. Define $c(P)$ by the same
family averaging of charged feature-plus-repair work. Within each bank,
select lexicographically by $(-m(P),-q(P),c(P),j(P))$, where $j$ is the original
slot. Gate success and actual scalar fit are reported separately; neither
guarantees schedule quality. Full-residual labels do not certify later
restricted-patch preferences. Budgets, banks, selection and ASTs freeze on
TRAIN before new evaluation; measured shared-DAG work remains distinct from
the additive catalogue cost in Theorem~2.
Charged work is an operation proxy, not machine-independent computation;
actual CPU and wall costs accompany deployment measurements.
Quality-only selections are separate comparison conditions: they maximize
$q(P)$ and then minimize $c(P)$ among completed feasible candidates without
the quotient gate. They are never counted as certified framework selections.
