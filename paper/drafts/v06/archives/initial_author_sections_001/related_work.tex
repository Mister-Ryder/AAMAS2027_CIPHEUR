\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Satellite scheduling couples observation, downlink and shared ground
resources~\cite{augenstein2016optimal,levinson2025optimal,picard2021constellations}.
Our contact-conflict formulation isolates a static pairwise scheduling
subproblem; it does not model every constellation constraint. Graph-based
learning can guide constructive optimization and exact
search~\cite{khalil2017learning,li2018gcn,gasse2019exact}, while local
topological information can improve neural generalization~\cite{bai2025local}.
These approaches motivate structural inputs. Our question is more specific:
when certified decisions conflict, can the current inputs express the
required preferences at all?

\paragraph{LLM-driven algorithm design.}
FunSearch combines generated programs with executable evaluation~\cite{romeraparedes2024funsearch}.
EoH evolves heuristic descriptions and code~\cite{liu2024eoh}; ReEvo adds
language-based reflection to evolutionary search~\cite{ye2024reevo}; LLaMEA
generates and evolves metaheuristics using fitness feedback~\cite{vanstein2025llamea}.
A$_2$DEPT evolves complete solver programs with hierarchical edits and
maintenance~\cite{chen2026a2dept}, while PathWise carries derivation history
through a search-memory graph and routed reflections~\cite{gungordu2026pathwise}.
These systems establish objective-driven algorithm synthesis. Their
open-ended programs can introduce structural computations, so feature
invention itself is not our distinction. We instead expose a certified
obstruction to a proposed input interface and use its concrete equality
joins to direct feature--rule refinement. Schedule quality and computation
cost remain necessary selection criteria: eliminating an information
obstruction does not imply a useful heuristic. DRAGON uses LLM decomposition
and reconstruction agents during instance optimization, passing local
metadata and boundary constraints~\cite{chen2026dragon}. Here the LLM acts
offline; a frozen typed program supplies priorities inside a classical
feasibility-enforcing kernel, without deployment-time model or conditional
oracle calls. This distinction concerns where reasoning occurs, not a
demonstrated advantage over DRAGON.
Dynamic heuristic selection conditions choices on search
state~\cite{speck2021dynamic}, and algorithm configuration optimizes settings
over instance distributions~\cite{hutter2009paramils}. We adapt the scorer's
available distinctions, while accounting for their deployment computation.

\paragraph{Representation and expressibility.}
Graph-neural-network expressiveness relates architectural distinctions to
graph isomorphism tests~\cite{xu2019powerful,morris2019weisfeiler}. Our quotient
instead identifies exactly equal feature vectors on a finite inventory of
decision occurrences. A directed cycle of strict requirements prevents any
deterministic pointwise scalar on that interface from satisfying them;
acyclicity permits an unrestricted scalar on those observed vectors. It
does not prove that our bounded rule grammar contains that scalar or that
it transfers to unseen graphs. Generalized planning already studies
representation change, feature learning and abstract actions shared across
instances~\cite{bonet2018representation,bonet2019features,frances2021policies}.
We specialize representation refinement to conditional optimization
preferences, with exact information conflicts and measured feature work.
Information adequacy and operational scheduling performance therefore require
separate empirical assessments.

\paragraph{Counterexample-guided and relational synthesis.}
CEGAR refines abstractions after detecting spurious
counterexamples~\cite{clarke2000cegar}; abstraction-guided synthesis similarly
refines program semantics~\cite{wang2018abstraction}. Sketching and
syntax-guided synthesis separate candidate syntax from semantic
obligations~\cite{solarlezama2006sketch,alur2013sygus}, and relational synthesis
constrains relationships among calls or programs~\cite{wang2018relational}.
Our counterexamples are sound conditional-preference requirements that
become contradictory after information is collapsed, rather than spurious
executions of a program abstraction. Refinement separates the responsible
occurrences before fitting a numerical ranking rule. The finite-catalogue
additive-cost repair result is scoped to fixed evidence and candidate
features; it neither certifies an LLM's search nor minimizes general
shared-computation runtime. These connections position the contribution
within established synthesis principles rather than claiming generic
counterexample-guided refinement as new.

\paragraph{Classical MWIS algorithms and evaluation.}
Independent-set exchanges~\cite{andrade2012local} and clique-based
branch-and-bound~\cite{ostergard2002clique} provide classical foundations.
Strong weighted solvers include concurrent local search in
CHILS~\cite{grossmann2025chils}, multilevel search in
M2WIS~\cite{grossmann2024mmwis}, increasing transformations in
Struction~\cite{gellner2021struction}, and weighted branch-and-reduce in
WeightedBR~\cite{lamm2019weighted}. Our bounded weighted repair is a shared
evaluation kernel, not a reproduction of these solvers or a new exact-search
primitive. Comparing authoring conditions within that kernel tests evidence
and representation contributions; comparisons with published solvers assess
complete algorithm performance separately. McCreesh et
al.~\cite{mccreesh2017evaluation} show that artificial weight assignments can
favor particular heuristics and inference rules. Their application-derived
benchmark collection motivates evaluating genuine weight structures as well
as scheduling graphs, with explicit graph transformations and numerical
compatibility rather than silently altering weights.
