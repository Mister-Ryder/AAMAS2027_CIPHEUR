\paragraph{Frozen-program follow-up.}
After the pilot, we evaluated the unchanged archived programs on 35 new
pairs: 12 diagnostic, 16 random, and 7 C3 pairs; one requested C3 window
was empty. These have no graph or original-contact overlap with any
pilot split. No program was resynthesized or selected using their outcomes.
All 70 references are exact; all 560 method--context schedules are feasible,
and all 112 C3 schedules pass the original verifier.
The joint program reaches 0.9968 overall, versus 0.9974 for rule-only.
Random ratios are 0.9988 versus 0.9973, while C3 ratios are 0.9868 versus
0.9934. Strict consistency remains higher, 61/64 versus 41/64,
but three certified inequalities are violated. This reversal of the
aggregate reward difference reinforces that representation adequacy and
complete-schedule quality must be evaluated separately. The follow-up
supports neither uniform reward superiority nor an LLM-specific advantage.
