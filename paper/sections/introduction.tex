\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Selecting a satellite--ground contact commits scarce communication resources
and removes competing opportunities. Its reward alone does not determine
its value: the reward forgone also depends on whether the excluded
alternatives could have been scheduled together. Ground-station switching
intervals and satellite resource constraints change these relationships,
even when contact windows and rewards remain fixed. Scheduling methods
already account for coupled operational resources~\cite{augenstein2016optimal,
levinson2025optimal,tao2023transmitting}. We study contact selection under
hard constraints represented by pairwise conflicts, where a reusable
maximum-weight independent set (MWIS) heuristic must respond to different
static resource configurations.

Large language models (LLMs) offer a way to synthesize such heuristics as
executable programs. FunSearch, EoH, and ReEvo search priority functions,
heuristic code, and reflective revisions using objective
feedback~\cite{romeraparedes2024funsearch,liu2024eoh,ye2024reevo}.
DRAGON uses decomposition and reconstruction agents with boundary
constraints and checker feedback during instance
optimization~\cite{chen2026dragon}. These approaches motivate a more
specific synthesis question: when a ranking failure reflects information
missing from the scoring interface, how should the synthesizer discover
and repair that interface? Repeatedly changing a formula cannot satisfy
incompatible strict rankings over identical observable inputs.

Figure~\ref{fig:motivation} illustrates the obstruction. Competing contacts
$a$ and $b$ each have reward $8$ and conflict with three peripheral
alternatives of reward $6$, yet those alternatives have different internal
conflicts. Before a
ground-station switching intervention, forcing $a$ or $b$ permits additional
completion rewards $20$ or $26$; afterward, the values are $20$ or $14$.
The certified preference changes from $b$ to $a$, while both remain
individually feasible and share the complete base feature vector within
each state. Every deterministic pointwise scorer of that vector assigns
them equal scores. Configuration fields shared by both actions do not
resolve the contradiction. A neighborhood-edge feature exposes the missing
distinction; these conditional values describe forced completions, not
the reward of an unrestricted heuristic rollout.

We propose certified representation--rule refinement: an offline synthesis
framework that turns decision evidence into explicit targets for structural
feature construction. Counterexample-guided refinement and learned planning
features provide established precedents~\cite{clarke2000cegar,
frances2021policies}. Sound conditional-completion bounds specify action
preferences under replayed commitments. Projecting the accumulated strict
relations onto exact feature-equivalence classes exposes directed cycles,
which certify an information obstruction for the current interface.
Concrete witnesses retain both ranking relations and the equality joins
that close each cycle. They direct LLM proposals toward graph operations
that distinguish the implicated occurrences. A finite feature master and
repeated full-quotient checks then select a cost-aware repair before rule
synthesis proceeds over the refined interface.

Comparisons replay actual incumbent rollout boundaries. Feasible schedules
supply lower bounds and clique envelopes bound the full residual completion
space; strict separation certifies reversals and preservations, while
unresolved queries remain unknown. Typed features compile into shared DAGs
with incremental residual aggregates. Selection charges actual compiled work
alongside complete-schedule reward and consistency, distinguishing deployment
cost from the additive repair surrogate.

Our contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Decision-relevant certified specifications.}
We develop rollout-anchored boundary replay and sound full-residual clique
bounds to certify reversal and preservation requirements across controlled
resource interventions.
\item \textbf{Witness-guided representation repair.}
We characterize finite pointwise representability by quotient acyclicity
and formulate equality-join separation as feature selection. Globally
solved masters terminating with an acyclic quotient minimize additive cost
over the fixed evidence, finite proposal catalogue, and declared feature limit.
\item \textbf{Executable joint refinement.}
We synthesize typed feature--rule pairs, share and incrementally maintain
their graph computations, and select frozen programs using specification
consistency, complete-schedule quality, and compiled computation cost.
\end{enumerate}

The fixed constructive kernel enforces feasibility throughout deployment.
Quotient acyclicity establishes representability of observed relations by
an unrestricted pointwise score; realizability in the permitted rule
language requires a separate check. Neither relational consistency nor
shared feature execution guarantees global schedule quality or
generalization. The evaluation therefore separates information repair,
decision relevance, discovery cost, and held-out quality--cost tradeoffs,
using independent synthesis requests with matched proposal counts.
