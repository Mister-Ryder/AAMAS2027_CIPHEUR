\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

A satellite--ground contact commits scarce resources and excludes competing
opportunities. Their forgone reward depends on whether they could coexist.
Resource thresholds change this relationship while contact windows and
rewards remain fixed. Operational scheduling models coupled
resources~\cite{augenstein2016optimal,levinson2025optimal,picard2021constellations}; we study
pairwise-conflict scheduling, where a reusable maximum-weight independent
set (MWIS) heuristic must respond to different static configurations.

LLMs synthesize executable heuristics using objective feedback in
FunSearch, EoH and ReEvo~\cite{romeraparedes2024funsearch,liu2024eoh,ye2024reevo}.
DRAGON uses decomposition/reconstruction agents with boundary constraints
and checker feedback~\cite{chen2026dragon}. We ask how synthesis can identify
missing inputs that make required rankings impossible: formula revision
cannot satisfy incompatible strict preferences over identical observations.

Figure~\ref{fig:motivation} constructs this obstruction. Actions $a,b$ have
equal rewards and base features. Their forced-completion increments are
$20,26$ before a ground-gap intervention and $20,14$ afterward. Both remain
feasible; the preference reverses, but every pointwise base scorer ties them
within each configuration. Shared resource fields cannot distinguish them;
neighborhood structure can. These values concern forced completions, not
an unrestricted rollout's reward.

We propose certified representation--rule refinement. Following abstraction
refinement and planning-feature precedents~\cite{clarke2000cegar,frances2021policies},
we project sound strict preferences onto exact feature-equivalence classes.
Directed cycles certify an information obstruction. Witnesses retain
ranking arcs and equality joins, targeting LLM proposals toward missing
structural distinctions. Full refined quotients are repeatedly checked:
separating one witness can leave other cycles intact.

Shared residual components cancel from value differences without bounding
their unknown optima. Reached argmax audits retain finite-pool regret bounds
and unknowns.
Demand-driven execution shares typed computations and charges work.
TRAIN selection balances complete-schedule reward and cost, with reached
regret ranking within a declared utility band. Our contributions are:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Certified information diagnosis:} finite pointwise
representability characterized by quotients and concrete cycle witnesses.
\item \textbf{Witness-guided repair:} full-quotient separation and minimum
additive-cost refinement over fixed evidence and a bounded finite catalogue.
\item \textbf{Decision-aware synthesis:} cancelled evidence, reached-action
audits and demanded computation support joint selection.
\end{enumerate}

Frozen scheduling and public-graph confirmation compare the selected
program with published MWIS implementations. A separate four-block,
144-proposal cold-authoring study contrasts explicit witnesses, certified
relations and objective feedback on new held-out configurations.
TRAIN-only selection precedes all confirmations. A fixed kernel enforces
graph feasibility without online LLM or oracle calls; quality and transfer
remain empirical.
