\section{Discussion and Limits}
\label{sec:discussion}

Designed probes certify temporal contradictions; 200 physical-source alias
queries remain unresolved. Public counterexamples demonstrate separate input
failures, without physical incidence or successful repair. The acyclic
primary interface still fits only 631/824 TRAIN rankings (788 actual-action,
36 prior): finite representability does not guarantee bounded-rule fit or transfer.
\balance

Repair optimality covers fixed evidence, a finite catalogue and additive
standalone costs. Shared cost and quality remain empirical. Finite-pool
regret excludes unqueried actions; cancellation narrows uncertainty without
new strict signs.

The four-block pilot has unmatched tokens/latency, small label gains and
little witness gain; exploratory transfer adds no general advantage.
Earlier authoring counts differ. Fresh confirmation excludes prior instances,
but C3 has one physical source. Hash weights/simple projections are declared
extensions. Encoded feasibility implies neither greedy optimality nor
published-solver dominance.
