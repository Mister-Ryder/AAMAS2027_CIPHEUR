\section{Discussion and Limits}
\label{sec:discussion}

Contradictions are certified on designed probes; all 200 broader natural
alias queries remain unresolved. Preference change alone is insufficient.
The primary interface is acyclic, yet its rule satisfies only 631/824 TRAIN
requirements (788 actual-action, 36 prior). The DAG gate implies unrestricted
finite representability, not bounded-rule fit or unseen consistency.

Repair optimality covers fixed evidence, a bounded finite catalogue and
additive standalone costs. Shared cost and schedule quality remain empirical.
Finite-pool regret excludes other feasible actions; unresolved bounds remain
unknown. Controlled cancellation narrows uncertainty without strict-yield gain.

Unequal proposal counts/checking effort and one authoring session preclude
model-level guidance claims. Fresh seeds/contacts prevent instance reuse,
but C3 remains one physical source. Public hash weights/simple projections
are declared extensions. Feasibility covers encoded predicates; results
establish no global greedy optimality or published-solver dominance.
