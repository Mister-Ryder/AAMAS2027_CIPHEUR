\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Scheduling and graph decisions.}
Mixed-integer decomposition~\cite{augenstein2016optimal}, integrated
collection/downlink planning~\cite{levinson2025optimal}, and ground-queue-aware
traffic scheduling~\cite{tao2023transmitting} establish resource context's
importance. Our pairwise model excludes arbitrary cumulative constraints.
Graph embeddings condition optimization on partial
solutions~\cite{khalil2017learning}; learned deferral changes independent-set
decision stages~\cite{ahn2020defer}. AAMAS work combines factor-graph inference
with routing~\cite{li2024factor} and local topology with neural
representations~\cite{bai2025local}. Structural inputs are established choices.
Published weighted branch-and-reduce~\cite{lamm2019weighted},
Struction~\cite{gellner2021struction}, memetic
kernelization~\cite{grossmann2024mmwis} and concurrent local
search~\cite{grossmann2025chils} provide our specialized comparators.

GNN expressiveness analyses characterize aggregation
indistinguishability~\cite{xu2019powerful,morris2019weisfeiler}. Our exact
declared-feature quotient instead certifies an obstruction for every
pointwise scalar scorer on observed occurrences; it is neither graph
isomorphism nor a bound on all policies. Structural witnesses also appear
in AAMAS universal motion-reachability theory~\cite{dhar2026universal},
addressing a different guarantee.

\paragraph{LLM algorithm discovery.}
FunSearch searches priorities inside fixed skeletons~\cite{romeraparedes2024funsearch};
EoH evolves thoughts/code~\cite{liu2024eoh}; ReEvo uses
reflection~\cite{ye2024reevo}. EoH-S optimizes complementary heuristic
sets~\cite{liu2026eohs}; LACE combines verified interfaces, tools and
runtime-constrained portfolio evolution~\cite{gong2026lace}. Offline discovery
and cost budgets are precedents. DRAGON's per-instance agents transmit
boundary constraints and checker feedback~\cite{chen2026dragon}. Our distinction
is diagnosing whether an interface can express certified conditional
rankings and targeting its missing structure. Zero online calls alone
establish no advantage.

\paragraph{Abstraction and synthesis.}
Counterexample-guided refinement uses failure witnesses~\cite{clarke2000cegar}.
Generalized planning learns symbolic features/actions~\cite{bonet2019features},
including feature selection and transition
separation~\cite{frances2021policies}; DreamCoder learns reusable components
with synthesis guidance~\cite{ellis2021dreamcoder}. We couple aligned resource
interventions and feasible boundary replay to sound rankings, equality-join
feature selection and repeated full-quotient separation. Acyclicity removes
observed information obstructions; bounded-rule fit, shared cost and
schedule quality remain distinct requirements.
