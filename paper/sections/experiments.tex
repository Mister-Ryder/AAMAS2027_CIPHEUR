\section{Experiments}

\subsection{Populations, Baselines and Metrics}
\label{sec:experiments:protocol}
TRAIN contains 33 pairs/66 contexts at 32--128 contacts. Frozen confirmation
uses 456 test contexts: 216 standard, 216 dense/long at 64--256 contacts,
and 24 C3 source blocks at 64--512. Another 132 validation contexts do not
select programs. Paired configurations preserve contacts, weights and windows
while changing one resource parameter. Test ground gaps $(0.5,6)$ shift from
TRAIN $(0,2)$; dense horizons shorten $1.8n\to0.18n$ and maximum duration
grows $12\to48$. Seeds/C3 opportunities exclude inventoried prior inputs.

Table~\ref{tab:baseline-v05} compares actually executed published implementations
with frozen synthesis and classical controls. CHILS-ILS uses the published
CHILS implementation's one-state ILS setting; it is not a separate algorithm.
Public transfer retains 18 DIMACS originals and 30 SATLIB instances,
each with unit/hash weights. Independent decoders verify clique complements
and literal-conflict reductions. These graph tasks do not encode satellite physics.
Free and rule-only synthesis are project controls, not EoH/ReEvo reproductions.

Competitive reward divides each outcome by its strongest saved verified feasible
witness, then averages contexts. Native seeds 1/2/3 are averaged first; failed
runs receive zero. Coverage uses all requested runs. A 100\% ratio does not
prove optimality; exact clique bounds and floating HiGHS endpoints remain separate.
Native solvers receive five seconds; constructive policies use a cooperative
five-process-CPU-second target, HiGHS ten seconds, and classical exchange two
after the best of five fixed-rule initializers. Exchange elapsed time includes
all five initialization attempts. Total computation is unmatched.
Paired source-cluster 95\% intervals use 2,000 bootstrap replicates, preserving
configurations/shared seeds and C3 blocks; public families/weight modes stay separate.

\subsection{What the LLM and Representation Contribute}
\label{sec:experiments:selection}
The original 93-program TRAIN bank contains 12 new guided proposals,
historical synthesis controls and fixed rules. One continuing assistant
session supplies the new proposals; unequal authoring counts/compute preclude
causal comparisons. TRAIN completes 5,996/6,138 assignments. Its primary normalizes weight
by the larger of own weight and a deterministic neighbor clique-cover envelope.
The regret tie-break and primary-only ablation select the identical AST.

The selected program improves Degree by 0.98 and 1.64 percentage points on
standard/dense scheduling, with positive paired intervals. Against rule-only
synthesis, gains are 0.54 [0.38,0.69] and 1.59 [0.88,2.30]. Free synthesis
has higher standard/dense point means; their paired differences cross zero.
C3 primary-minus-Free is +0.175 [0.036,0.346], while the Degree and rule-only
contrasts cross zero. Thus the evidence supports some benefits of this selected
structural rule, without establishing an overall advantage attributable to LLM
guidance. Published solvers and classical exchange attain higher fresh reward;
several native implementations also terminate faster (Table~\ref{tab:baseline-v05}).
For example, Struction's median elapsed seconds are 0.009/0.015/0.012
on standard/dense/C3, versus primary's 0.209/0.235/0.474. This reports
observed costs under different prescribed budgets, not equal-compute dominance.

Representation evidence differs in scope. Constructed probes certify
obstructions; 200 physical-source alias queries remain unresolved.
Pure TRAIN cancellation fixes unmatched bounds on 396 comparisons: all
intervals nest, 199 narrow, and pooled median width falls $86.75\to36$.
Table~\ref{tab:cancellation-strata} exposes the mechanism's boundary.
In standard TRAIN, 197 of 216 comparisons narrow, but none acquires a strict
sign. Dense/long retains 17 strict certificates without narrowing; C3 has
two narrower intervals and unchanged strict yield of 16. Removing common
uncertainty exactly can leave the unmatched continuation's sign unresolved.
A separate $10\to33$ strict-yield comparison changes the bounding strategy
and does not isolate cancellation. Catalogue repair proves neither natural
incidence nor unrestricted minimality.

\paragraph{Matched cold-authoring pilot.}
Four independent authoring blocks compare A (relations plus equality-join
witnesses), B (relations) and C (objective feedback). All arms receive the
same graphs, typed grammar, limits and 12 slots; tokens/latency are unmatched.
The complete 144-slot bank freezes before TRAIN assessment. Each arm uses
the same $J$ selector, then evaluates its four frozen winners on 216 new
standard/dense contexts, disjoint from inventoried prior inputs.
Table~\ref{tab:matched-llm} uses a common independently verified clique
upper bound, not the competitive-reference denominator above.

All 2,808 TEST assignments complete. Relations improve four-block mean
quality over objective feedback by 0.657 points, with positive differences
in three blocks and one reversal. Explicit witnesses add only 0.014 points
over relations (two ties). Figure~\ref{fig:llm-pilot} shows full-gate yields
48/48, 48/48 and 40/48; all actual-only quotients are acyclic, so the yield
contrast arises from included prior diagnostic requirements. No candidate
fits all 824 scalar rankings. This pilot supports a small descriptive
label-guidance benefit, not strong witness superiority or a model-population
effect; it contains no LLM-versus-non-LLM causal arm.

\subsection{Progress Curves and Transfer Limits}
\label{sec:experiments:evidence}
Figure~\ref{fig:effect-curves} resolves aggregate gains by size and resource
regime. Dense satellite-scarce $n=128$ gains over rule-only are
4.05 [1.37,7.31] points, whereas ground-scarce $n=256$ gives
0.03 [$-2.08$,2.42]. Actual rollout-prefix curves lag Degree/rule-only early
and recover a positive final mean; the Free contrast remains inconclusive.
Prefixes are indexed by eliminated-vertex fraction, not elapsed time or LLM
learning iterations. These retrospective diagnostics replay unchanged frozen
ASTs and do not supply unrecorded native anytime trajectories.

Primary completion is 456/456 fresh contexts, 34/36 DIMACS and 20/60 SATLIB;
public quality losses include timeout failures rather than selecting successful
graphs. CHILS, ILS and Struction complete all 288 public short seed runs;
M$^2$WIS/WeightedBR fail six/eight. Separate prescribed 30-second subsets
have 20 fresh and 21 public contexts; native methods complete all runs,
while public primary completes 18/21 at its unchanged CPU target.
Four SNAP simple projections (eight weight variants, 4,039--12,008 vertices)
are a separate extension: original primary completion is 0/8.
The SATLIB failures are concentrated: all ten completed cases per weight
mode are \texttt{uf20-91}, while all \texttt{uf50-218}/\texttt{uf75-325}
cases time out. Successful-subset competitive reward is 98.79\%/93.14\%,
but its failure-zero population means are 32.93\%/31.05\%. Conditional
quality would hide a source-family selection effect; Degree completes all
thirty cases. On DIMACS unit weights, primary also trails Degree among
completed cases, so computation alone does not explain transfer loss.
Completed C3 schedules pass the original physical verifier. A fixed-AST
588-context audit finds primary finite-pool regret positive/zero/unknown in
18/191/346 actually reached sampled test states; this is not all-action or
whole-schedule regret.

\subsection{Execution Adaptation}
\label{sec:experiments:deployment}
Demanded/full-interface traces match on all 456 fresh contexts. A separate
same-AST heap extension preserves all 2,736 assessable fresh pairs;
mean context-median full-scan/heap CPU ratios
are $15.45\times$ [12.91,18.19], $1.19\times$ [1.15,1.23] and
$4.61\times$ [4.09,5.19] on standard/dense/C3. Degree slows on dense
($0.87\times$). Sparse full scans complete 0/24 per AST; five-second heaps
complete 7/24 primary and 18/24 Degree, increasing separately to 12/24 and
24/24 at thirty seconds. With no successful full-scan pairs, sparse speedup
and backend parity remain unassessable. These execution gains do not measure
LLM benefit or replace failed original confirmations (Figure~\ref{fig:heap-scaling}).

\paragraph{Frozen-winner transfer.}
An exploratory extension executes the 12 cold-authoring winners and Degree
on 120 earlier public/C3 contexts, retaining prior verified upper bounds
and no reselection. All use the same compiled backend and cooperative
five-CPU-second target after AST parsing. Table~\ref{tab:matched-transfer}
reports 1,560 assignments: 1,007 complete and 553 time out.
Witness guidance has no general transfer advantage. All synthesized arms
complete SATLIB at 273 vertices and fail at 654/975; Degree also completes
654. The Witness block with extra neighbor packing has five C3 timeouts.
Objective's C3 quality exceeds compiled Degree by 0.758 points of $U$,
but reaches only 98.12\% of the prior feasible reference. Reused contexts
make this a diagnostic extension, not independent confirmation.
