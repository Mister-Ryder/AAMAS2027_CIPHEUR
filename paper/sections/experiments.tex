\subsection{Populations and Frozen Protocol}
\label{sec:experiments:protocol}
TRAIN has 33 pairs/66 contexts at 32--128 contacts. Fresh confirmation uses
456 test contexts; 132 validation contexts do not select programs
(Table~\ref{tab:study-protocol}). Pairs preserve contacts, weights and windows
while changing one resource parameter. Three resource regimes share seeds.
Test ground gaps $(0.5,6)$ shift from TRAIN $(0,2)$; dense horizons shorten
$1.8n\to0.18n$ and maximum duration grows $12\to48$.

Seeds/C3 opportunities are split-disjoint and exclude inventoried prior inputs.
Public transfer uses 18 DIMACS originals and 30 SATLIB instances with
unit/hash weights; independent decoders check clique complements and
literal-conflict reductions. SNAP projects four sources to simple graphs.

Pinned CHILS, M$^2$WIS, Struction and weighted branch-and-reduce
executables~\cite{grossmann2025chils,grossmann2024mmwis,gellner2021struction,lamm2019weighted}
have verified output mappings and seeds 1/2/3. CHILS uses four population
states and a one-state ILS control. Native budgets are five seconds or 30 on
prespecified subsets. Constructive policies retain a cooperative
five-process-CPU-second target; HiGHS receives ten seconds and classical
one-to-two exchange search two after initialization. Computation is unmatched.

Null failures receive zero all-assigned reward; coverage accompanies conditional
summaries. Competitive reward averages native seeds within contexts and divides
by the strongest verified saved feasible witness, without establishing optimality.
Formal clique-upper ratios remain separate from floating HiGHS bounds.
The 2,000-replicate 95\% bootstrap retains paired configurations/shared seeds
across regimes and C3 source clusters; public family/weight modes remain separate.
Wall points average native seeds; standalone exchange-search cost includes initialization.

All 64 original strict self-loops come from probes; 19 non-probe specifications
form an acyclic quotient and 200 broader alias queries remain unresolved.
Catalogue repair proves neither natural incidence nor unrestricted minimality;
full-quotient checks detect cycles surviving coarse-witness repair.

Pure TRAIN cancellation fixes unmatched bounds on 396 comparisons. All intervals
remain nested; 199 narrow, with median width $86.75\to36$ reward units.
Strict certificates remain $33\to33$. The separate whole-residual/component
$10\to33$ comparison changes strategy and does not isolate cancellation.

\subsection{Frozen Selection and Confirmation}
\label{sec:experiments:selection}
The 93-program bank contains 12 new guided proposals, 24 each from existing
free/rule-only/enumeration arms, five classical rules and four prior guided
controls. One Codex session (\texttt{gpt-6.1-sol}, ultra) authors new proposals
without external API calls. Unequal counts and unavailable tokens preclude model-level
or matched-compute guidance claims.

TRAIN completes 5,996/6,138 assignments. The selected rule is
\[
 h(v)=\frac{w_v}{\max\{10^{-6},w_v,\mathrm{nc}_v\}}.
\]
Here $\mathrm{nc}$ is the deterministic
weighted clique-cover envelope of active neighbors. Both guided
selections coincide; the secondary criterion has no observed effect.

Retrospective six-program auditing covers 588 contexts. Across 555 sampled
primary reached test states, finite-pool regret is positive in 18, zero in 191
and unresolved in 346; counterfactual states do not enter these counts.

Primary completion is 456/456 fresh contexts. Competitive reward is
99.46\% [99.36,99.55] on standard, 96.11\% [95.58,96.62] on dense and
97.56\% [96.57,98.48] C3 (Figure~\ref{fig:quality-cost-fresh}).
Degree-rule advantages are 0.98 percentage points [0.79,1.17] and
1.64 [0.93,2.36] temporally; the C3 interval crosses zero.
CHILS, ILS, M$^2$WIS and Struction attain 100\% descriptive competitive reward.
M$^2$WIS, Struction and weighted branch-and-reduce terminate faster; no published-solver
superiority follows. Older continuation fails 211/456. Completed C3 schedules
pass the original physical verifier; only two fresh contexts attain the clique upper.

Public primary completion is 34/36 DIMACS and 20/60 SATLIB. Unit-weight
competitive rewards are 80.53\% and 32.93\%; hash-weight rewards are
87.07\% and 31.05\%, respectively (Figure~\ref{fig:quality-cost-public}). CHILS, ILS and Struction complete
288/288 short seed runs; M$^2$WIS and weighted branch-and-reduce fail six/eight.
All four native methods complete 63/63 subset runs; primary completion is
18/21 at unchanged CPU target. SNAP primary completion is 0/8 versus
24/24 native seeds each except weighted branch-and-reduce, 21/24.

\subsection{Execution Cost}
\label{sec:experiments:deployment}
Demanded/full-interface primary traces match on 456 fresh contexts.
A separate post-diagnostic heap extension preserves frozen ASTs and all
2,736 assessable fresh pairs (Figure~\ref{fig:heap-execution}). Mean
context-median full-scan/heap CPU ratios are $15.45\times$ [12.91,18.19],
$1.19\times$ [1.15,1.23] and $4.61\times$ [4.09,5.19] on standard/dense/C3.
Degree slows on dense graphs ($0.87\times$). SNAP full scans complete
0/24 repeats per AST; heap completes 7/24 primary and 18/24 degree runs.
Sparse parity/speedups remain unassessable; failed-run quality receives no
credit in original confirmation.
