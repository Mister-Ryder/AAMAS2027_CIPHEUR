\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We connect certified conditional scheduling preferences to representation
refinement. Exact quotient contradictions expose lost distinctions;
concrete equality-join witnesses direct typed feature synthesis; and
full-quotient separation selects economical repairs within a finite
catalogue. Budgeted full-residual bounds supply reversal and preservation
evidence, while complete schedules and compiled work guide feature--rule
selection. The resulting frozen program adapts to the current conflict
graph inside a feasibility-enforcing kernel, without online LLM or oracle
calls. This framework makes information adequacy an explicit synthesis
target and separates its guarantees from empirical scheduling performance.
